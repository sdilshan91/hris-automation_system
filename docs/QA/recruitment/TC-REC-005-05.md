# TC-REC-005-05: Multiple interview rounds -- Round 1 and Round 2 for the same applicant are tracked independently with their own interviewers, schedules, and scorecards (AC-4, FR-2)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-4 / FR-2: a recruiter can schedule a second interview (Round 2) for the same applicant on the same vacancy, with DIFFERENT interviewers and a DIFFERENT schedule, and both rounds are tracked as SEPARATE `interview` records (distinct round_number, own interviewer junction rows, own status, own reminder job, own scorecard association). Editing/cancelling one round does not affect the other.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-005
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-4
- Functional Requirements: FR-2 (multiple rounds, separate record + round number)
- Data Requirements: S7 (`interview.round_number`; independent `interview_interviewer` rows per round)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant acme; applicant "Jordan Rivera" at "Interview" with a Round 1 interview already scheduled (empA, empB).
- Two additional active employees {empC}, {empD} available as Round 2 interviewers.
- Recruiter with `Recruitment.Manage.All` authenticated.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Round 1 | round_number=1, interviewers [empA,empB], 2026-06-25 10:00 | Existing |
| Round 2 | round_number=2, interviewers [empC,empD], 2026-06-30 11:00 | New, different interviewers + time |
| Vacancy | Senior Backend Engineer | Same vacancy, same applicant |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | `POST /api/v1/recruitment/interviews` for the same applicant/vacancy with interviewers [empC,empD], date 2026-06-30 11:00, round explicit or auto-incremented | 201; a SECOND `interview` row created with round_number=2, status=Scheduled, its own hangfire_job_id (AC-4, FR-2). |
| 2 | Verify independence of records | Two distinct interview rows exist for the applicant: round 1 (empA,empB) and round 2 (empC,empD); junction rows do not overlap. |
| 3 | Verify Round 2 notifications + reminder | empC, empD, and the applicant receive a Round 2 creation notification; a separate reminder job is scheduled for 2026-06-29 11:00. |
| 4 | Cancel Round 1 | Round 1 -> Cancelled and its reminder removed; Round 2 is UNAFFECTED (still Scheduled with its own reminder) (FR-2 independence). |
| 5 | View the applicant timeline | Both rounds appear as separate interview cards with their own interviewers, type badges, and statuses (AC-4). |
| 6 | Verify scorecard association seam | Each round can carry its own scorecard(s) (US-REC-006 seam); a Round 1 scorecard is not attributed to Round 2. |

## 6. Postconditions
- The applicant has two independent interview rounds; lifecycle actions on one round never affect the other.
