# TC-REC-006-04: Recruiter applicant-detail view shows individual criterion scores + overall average + written feedback

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-2 / FR-8: after an interviewer submits, a recruiter viewing the applicant detail panel sees the scorecard with (a) each individual criterion score (1-5), (b) the overall average score for that scorecard, (c) the interviewer's written feedback (per-criterion comments + general notes), and (d) the overall recommendation. When multiple scorecards exist, they are shown side-by-side with the aggregate highlighted (the consolidated math itself is covered in TC-REC-006-03; this TC is the recruiter-facing read surface and field completeness).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-006
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-8, FR-1 (recommendation labels)
- Business Rules: (none specific; recruiter is non-anonymized per story assumption -- can see which interviewer scored what)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" active; a recruiter with Recruitment.Read.All is authenticated.
- empA has submitted a scorecard for an applicant's interview {acme_interviewId} (scores 4/5/3/4, comments present, recommendation=Hire).

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Recruiter | recruiter@acme | Recruitment.Read.All |
| Applicant | Jordan Rivera | At Interview stage |
| empA card | 4/5/3/4, Hire, comments | Already submitted |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As recruiter, open the applicant detail panel for Jordan Rivera | The interview scorecard section is visible (AC-2). |
| 2 | Inspect individual criterion scores | Each of the 4 criteria shows its 1-5 score (Technical 4, Communication 5, Problem Solving 3, Cultural Fit 4) with its label (e.g. 4=Good) (FR-1). |
| 3 | Inspect the overall average | The card's overall average (4.0) is displayed (FR-8). |
| 4 | Inspect written feedback | empA's per-criterion comments and general notes are shown verbatim (server-side sanitized -- see TC-REC-006-13 for the a11y/render side; XSS handled where rich text is rendered). |
| 5 | Inspect overall recommendation | recommendation=Hire is shown with its color coding (green/light-green/orange/red mapping). |
| 6 | Multiple-scorecard view | When a second interviewer has also submitted, both cards render side-by-side with the aggregate average highlighted (consolidated math: TC-REC-006-03). |
| 7 | Anti-bias is NOT applied to the recruiter | The recruiter (Read.All, not an assigned interviewer) sees ALL submitted cards regardless of submission state -- the anti-bias hide (FR-6/BR-5) applies only to OTHER interviewers (TC-REC-006-05), not to recruiters. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Recruiter sees individual criterion scores, overall average, recommendation, and written feedback for every submitted scorecard on the applicant.
