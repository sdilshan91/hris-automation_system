# TC-REC-007-05: Offer expiry -> Hangfire reminder, then status Expired after grace period (no response by expiry)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify the expiry lifecycle (AC-4, FR-7, BR-6): a `Sent` offer that receives no response triggers (a) a Hangfire reminder notification to the recruiter (and optionally the applicant) ahead of / at the expiry date, and (b) after the configurable grace period elapses with still no response, the expiry auto-status Hangfire job changes the offer status `Sent -> Expired`. The jobs are tenant-aware (carry tenant_id) and idempotent (re-running does not double-notify or re-expire). This is the time-driven branch that the accept/decline paths (TC-REC-007-03/04) cancel.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-007
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-4
- Functional Requirements: FR-6 (Expired status), FR-7 (expiry jobs)
- Business Rules: BR-6 (expiry mandatory; default +7d; configurable grace)
- Non-Functional: NFR-4 (async)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" active; a `Sent` offer {acme_offerId} with reminder + expiry jobs scheduled (TC-REC-007-02); applicant has NOT responded.
- Hangfire (S28) wired, OR an enqueue/clock-advance harness is available to fire the jobs (see Conditional note).
- Reminder lead time + grace period configured per tenant.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Offer | {acme_offerId} | status Sent, no response |
| Expiry date | T (near-future for test) | drives auto-status job |
| Reminder lead | 2 days before T | reminder job |
| Grace period | configurable (e.g. 0-1 day after T) | before auto-Expire |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Advance the clock / trigger the reminder job at (expiry - lead time) with no response recorded | A reminder notification is queued to the recruiter (and optionally the applicant) via Hangfire/outbox; offer status still `Sent` (AC-4, FR-7). |
| 2 | Verify reminder idempotency + tenant-awareness | Re-firing the reminder job does not duplicate the notification; the job carries tenant_id + offer id and resolves only acme data (NFR-4). |
| 3 | Advance the clock past expiry + grace with still no response; trigger the expiry auto-status job | Offer status `Sent -> Expired`; responded_at remains null; the applicant is NOT auto-rejected (stays where they are unless the recruiter acts) (AC-4, FR-6, BR-6). |
| 4 | Verify auto-status idempotency | Re-firing the expiry job on an already-`Expired` offer is a no-op; no double transition. |
| 5 | Verify response wins over expiry | If a response arrives BEFORE the expiry job fires, the offer becomes Accepted/Declined and the expiry job no-ops (the accept/decline path cancels the job, TC-REC-007-03/04). |
| 6 | Verify an Expired offer frees a new offer | Because Expired is not "active" (BR-2), the recruiter may generate a new offer for the same applicant/vacancy. |

## 6. Postconditions
- With no response, offer {acme_offerId} transitions Sent -> (reminder) -> Expired after grace; jobs idempotent + tenant-scoped; a new offer may be generated.
