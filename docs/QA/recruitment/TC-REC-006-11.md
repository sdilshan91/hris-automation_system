# TC-REC-006-11: A submitted scorecard satisfies REC-004's Interview->Offer gate criterion

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-6 (cross-story integration with US-REC-004, FR-1): a submitted interview scorecard serves as the gate criterion for advancing an applicant to the Offer stage. With at least one submitted scorecard on the applicant's interview, the Interview->Offer transition's scorecard gate is SATISFIED (no warning); with zero scorecards, the soft gate WARNS (overridable by Manage, per REC-004's soft-gate contract in TC-REC-004-04). This closes the seam REC-004 left CONDITIONAL on US-REC-006.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-006
- Business Rules: BR-6
- Cross-story: US-REC-004 FR-1 / BR-1 (soft gate), TC-REC-004-04
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1 (the scorecard that satisfies the gate)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" active; applicant Jordan Rivera is at the Interview stage with an interview {acme_interviewId}.
- A recruiter with Recruitment.Manage.All is authenticated.
- The REC-004 gate evaluator is wired (this TC verifies the now-delivered scorecard data source; if the evaluator is still stubbed, see Conditional note).

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Scenario A (no scorecard): attempt Interview->Offer for Jordan with ZERO submitted scorecards | The Offer gate WARNS "no interview scorecard submitted" (soft gate, REC-004 FR-1); a Manager may override; the transition is not silently allowed without acknowledgement. |
| 2 | empA submits a valid scorecard for {acme_interviewId} (4/4/4/4, Hire) | 201; one scorecard exists for the applicant's interview (AC-1). |
| 3 | Scenario B: re-evaluate the Interview->Offer gate | The scorecard gate is now SATISFIED -- no warning is raised for the scorecard criterion; the transition proceeds without an override prompt (BR-6). |
| 4 | Verify "at least one" semantics | One submitted scorecard suffices to satisfy the gate even on a multi-interviewer interview (story assumption: no minimum count enforced; the gate requires >=1). |
| 5 | Verify a non-submitted (draft/missing-recommendation) card does NOT satisfy the gate | Only a VALID submitted card (recommendation present) counts; an incomplete/rejected submission leaves the gate unsatisfied (ties to TC-REC-006-07). |

## 6. Postconditions
- The Interview->Offer scorecard gate warns with zero scorecards and is satisfied once at least one valid scorecard is submitted.
