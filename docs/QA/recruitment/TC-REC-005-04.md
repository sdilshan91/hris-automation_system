# TC-REC-005-04: Cancel an interview -> status Cancelled, reminder job removed, cancellation notifications to all participants; applicant pipeline stage NOT changed (AC-3, BR-4)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-3 / BR-4 / FR-3 / FR-6: cancelling a scheduled interview (a) sets status=Cancelled (retaining the record, not deleting it), (b) REMOVES the pending Hangfire reminder job (no reminder will fire), (c) sends a cancellation notification (email + in-app) to all participants, and CRITICALLY (d) does NOT change the applicant's pipeline stage -- per BR-4 the recruiter must explicitly reject or reschedule; cancellation alone leaves the applicant in "Interview". This is the cancel half of AC-3 (TC-REC-005-03 covers reschedule).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-005
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-3
- Functional Requirements: FR-3 (cancellation notification), FR-6 (status=Cancelled)
- Business Rules: BR-4 (cancel does NOT change pipeline stage), BR-6 (reminder removed), BR-7 (recipients)

## 3. Preconditions
- A Scheduled interview exists in acme with a pending reminder job and applicant at "Interview".
- Recruiter with `Recruitment.Manage.All` authenticated in acme.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Interview | {interviewId} | Scheduled, has pending reminder |
| Applicant stage before | Interview | Must be unchanged after cancel (BR-4) |
| Participants | empA, empB, applicant | Cancellation notified (BR-7) |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | `POST /api/v1/recruitment/interviews/{interviewId}/cancel` (or status->Cancelled) | 200; interview status=Cancelled; the record is retained (not hard-deleted), tenant_id=acme. |
| 2 | Verify reminder removed | The pending Hangfire reminder job is cancelled/deleted; no reminder will fire for this interview (BR-6). |
| 3 | Verify cancellation notifications | Cancellation email + in-app notification sent to empA, empB, AND the applicant (FR-3, BR-7). |
| 4 | Verify pipeline stage unchanged | The applicant is STILL at "Interview"; no `applicant_stage_history` row was written by the cancellation (BR-4) -- the recruiter must separately reject/reschedule. |
| 5 | Attempt to cancel an already-Cancelled interview | Rejected/no-op (idempotent); no second cancellation notification, no error corrupting state. |
| 6 | Verify the cancelled interview stays visible/auditable | The Cancelled interview still appears in the applicant timeline + calendar (color-coded gray per UI notes), preserving history. |

## 6. Postconditions
- The interview is Cancelled with its reminder removed; all participants were notified; the applicant's pipeline stage is unchanged.
