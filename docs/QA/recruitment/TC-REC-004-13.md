# TC-REC-004-13: Hired is a terminal stage -- no stage move OUT of Hired is allowed (BR-3) [regression: BUG-059]

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-3 (Hired is terminal + irreversible): once an applicant reaches "Hired", the pipeline is finished and NO stage move out of Hired is permitted -- neither a backward move (e.g. Hired -> Offer) nor a rejection (Hired -> Rejected). The onward handling of a hire is the convert-to-employee flow (US-REC-010), not a pipeline stage move. Regression coverage for **BUG-059**: pre-fix, a Hired applicant could be moved backward to any stage (a supplied reason satisfied every existing gate), silently un-hiring them.

> Traceability note: the fix commit labels BUG-059 "US-REC-008", but the governing acceptance criterion is US-REC-004 **BR-3 (Hired terminal + irreversible)**. US-REC-008 is the candidate-portal story and has no stage-transition AC. This TC is traced to US-REC-004 to satisfy AC linkage.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-004

## 3. Preconditions

- An applicant exists at stage "Hired" on an Open vacancy.

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Move the Hired applicant -> Offer with a reason | 400 `hired_is_terminal`; the move is blocked; stage stays "Hired"; no `applicant_stage_history` row written. |
| 2 | Move the Hired applicant -> Rejected with reason + structured rejectionReason | 400 `hired_is_terminal`; even a rejection cannot leave the terminal stage; stage stays "Hired". |
| 3 | Control: move an applicant INTO Hired (Offer -> Hired) | 200; moving into Hired remains allowed -- the guard blocks moves OUT of Hired, not into it. |
| 4 | Control: a normal non-Hired forward move (Screening -> Interview) | 200; unrelated transitions are unaffected by the terminal guard. |

## 6. Postconditions
- The Hired applicant is unchanged; the terminal invariant holds.
