# TC-REC-004-15: Advancing an applicant to the Interview stage without a scheduled interview surfaces a SOFT (non-blocking) warning; a scheduled interview clears it (ISSUE-108 — BR-1)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify the ISSUE-108 fix on US-REC-004 FR-1/BR-1: the Interview-stage gate is a **soft** control. Moving an applicant to `Interview` with **no** interview on record still succeeds, but surfaces a non-blocking warning ("...interview...scheduled..."). When at least one interview is on record, the gate is satisfied and no interview warning is returned. The move is never blocked by this gate.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-004
- Functional Requirement: FR-1 (stage transitions)
- Business Rule: BR-1 (interview gate is a soft warning, not a hard block)
- Finding: ISSUE-108 (PR #371)

## 3. Preconditions
- An applicant at the `Screening` stage in the tenant.
- Uses the EF Core InMemory provider through `ApplicantStageMoveService` (mirrors `ApplicantStageMoveServiceTests`).
