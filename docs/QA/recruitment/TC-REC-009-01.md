# TC-REC-009-01: Recruitment dashboard loads with correct KPI cards (open vacancies, total applicants, hires, avg time-to-hire, offer acceptance rate, offers pending) for the default period (happy path)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify the primary dashboard flow (AC-1, FR-1): an authenticated HR Officer with `Recruitment.Read.All` + `Reports.View.All` navigates to the Recruitment Dashboard and sees the KPI card row populated with correct, tenant-scoped values for the default selected period. Each KPI is asserted against a seeded, known dataset: Open Vacancies (count of vacancies in `Open` status), Total Applicants (applicants whose `applied_at` falls in the period), Hires (applicants who entered the `Hired` stage in the period), Average Time-to-Hire in calendar days computed per BR-1 (`hired_at - applied_at`), Offer Acceptance Rate per BR-2 (`accepted / sent * 100`), and Offers Pending (offers currently in `Sent` status awaiting response). Trend arrows compare to the previous equal-length period (UI/UX S8).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-009
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1
- Functional Requirements: FR-1 (KPI cards)
- Business Rules: BR-1 (time-to-hire = days from applied_at to Hired), BR-2 (offer acceptance rate)
- Non-Functional Requirements: NFR-2 (tenant-scoped)
- Data Requirements: S7 (aggregates from `applicant`, `applicant_stage_history`, `vacancy`, `offer`)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" active; user authenticated with `Recruitment.Read.All` + `Reports.View.All`.
- Seeded acme dataset for the default period (e.g. last 30 days): 5 vacancies Open (others Draft/Closed), 100 applicants with `applied_at` in-period, 5 applicants advanced to `Hired` in-period with known applied/hired dates summing to an average of 24 days, 10 offers Sent of which 7 Accepted, 2 offers currently Sent/pending.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Subdomain | acme.yourhrm.com | Resolves tenant context |
| Open vacancies | 5 | Only `Open` status counted |

| Hires (period) | 5 | entered `Hired` in period |
| Avg time-to-hire | 24 days | mean of (hired_at - applied_at), BR-1 |
| Offers sent / accepted | 10 / 7 | acceptance rate = 70% (BR-2) |
