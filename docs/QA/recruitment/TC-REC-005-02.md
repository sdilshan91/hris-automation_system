# TC-REC-005-02: Hangfire reminder fires ~24h before the interview, sending email + in-app reminder to ALL participants; job is idempotent and tenant-aware

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-2 / FR-4 / NFR-4: the scheduled Hangfire reminder job fires at the configured lead time (default 24h) before the interview start and dispatches a reminder (email + in-app) to every participant -- all interviewers AND the applicant. The job runs in the correct tenant context (tenant_id from job parameters) and is IDEMPOTENT: a re-execution (Hangfire retry) does not send duplicate reminders. Notifications are async (Hangfire), so this is an integration/timing test; if delivery is a log/outbox stub, assert the outbox/log record instead of a real email.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-005
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-4
- Non-Functional Requirements: NFR-3 (async), NFR-4 (idempotent, tenant-aware)
- Business Rules: BR-5 (configurable lead time), BR-7 (recipients)

## 3. Preconditions
- A Scheduled interview from TC-REC-005-01 exists in acme with hangfire_job_id and two interviewers + an applicant.
- Tenant reminder lead time = 24h (default); a second tenant "globex" exists to confirm tenant scoping.

| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Interview start | 2026-06-25 10:00 (acme) | From TC-REC-005-01 |
| Expected fire time | 2026-06-24 10:00 | start - 24h (BR-5) |
| Recipients | empA, empB, applicant | All participants (BR-7) |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Advance/simulate the clock to the job fire time (or trigger the enqueued job directly) | The Hangfire reminder job executes at start - 24h (2026-06-24 10:00) (AC-2). |
| 2 | Verify recipients | Reminder email + in-app notification delivered/queued to empA, empB, AND the applicant -- all participants, none missed (FR-4, BR-7). |
| 3 | Verify tenant context | The job ran under acme's tenant context (tenant_id pulled from job parameters); notification content + lookups are acme-scoped (NFR-4). |
| 4 | Re-run the job (simulate Hangfire automatic retry / duplicate dispatch) | Idempotent: no duplicate reminders are sent for the same interview occurrence (NFR-4). |
| 5 | Configure globex lead time = 48h and schedule a globex interview | Its reminder fires at start - 48h, proving the lead time is tenant-configurable (BR-5) and per-tenant. |
| 6 | Verify async (non-blocking) | Reminder dispatch happens entirely on the Hangfire worker, never on a user request thread (NFR-3). |

## 6. Postconditions
- Each participant received exactly one reminder for the interview at the tenant-configured lead time; the tenant-aware job is safe to retry.
