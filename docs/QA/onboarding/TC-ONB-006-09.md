# TC-ONB-006-09: Authorization — HR required to record/view analytics; self-service restricted to the employee's own offboarding; 401/403 deny

## 1. Test Objective
Verify FR-2/FR-5 authorization: recording an HR-conducted exit interview and viewing exit interview analytics require the appropriate HR permission (e.g. `Offboarding.Manage` / analytics view); a self-service interview can only be submitted by the employee for their OWN offboarding. Unauthenticated requests get 401; authenticated-but-unauthorized requests get 403; no record is created on denial.

## 2. Related Requirements
- US-ONB-006
- Functional Requirements: FR-2 (HR-conducted vs self-service modes), FR-5 (permission gating)
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2, AC-3, AC-4

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant `acme` with: HR Officer (has record + analytics permission); a regular employee E300 (own offboarding OB-A300); a different employee E302 (own offboarding OB-A302); an anonymous client.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| HR user | acme HR Officer | authorized |
| employee E300 | owns OB-A300 | self-service own only |
| employee E302 | owns OB-A302 | must not touch OB-A300 |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Anonymous: call the record-interview and analytics endpoints | 401 Unauthorized; no data returned or written. |
| 2 | Regular employee E300: attempt to record an hr_conducted interview / open analytics | 403 Forbidden — lacks HR permission; no record created. |
| 3 | HR Officer: record an hr_conducted interview and open analytics | Permitted (200/201) (AC-2, AC-4). |
| 4 | Employee E302: attempt to submit a self-service interview for OB-A300 (not their own) | 403/404 — self-service restricted to own offboarding; no record created (FR-2). |
| 5 | Employee E300: submit a self-service interview for their own OB-A300 | Permitted (AC-3). |
| 6 | Verify denial side effects | No interview record, task-completion, or audit event created for any denied request. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Only HR may record-on-behalf and view analytics; self-service is scoped to the employee's own offboarding; denials leave no side effects.
