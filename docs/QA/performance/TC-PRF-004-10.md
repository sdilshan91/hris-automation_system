# TC-PRF-004-10: Rating scale locks once the cycle transitions from Draft to Active (BR-5)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-5: the rating scale is freely selectable while the cycle is Draft, but becomes locked (immutable) once the cycle transitions to Active, so participants are never rated against a scale that changes mid-cycle.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PRF-004
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1 (rating scale selection), AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-6, FR-7
- Business Rules: BR-5

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" active with at least two configured rating scales (e.g. 1-5 and 1-10).
- HR Officer Maya authenticated with cycle permissions.
- Draft cycle "FY26 Annual Review" with rating scale 1-5 selected.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Scale A | 1-5 | initial Draft selection |
| Scale B | 1-10 | attempted change |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | While the cycle is Draft, change the rating scale from 1-5 to 1-10 and save | 200; the change is accepted (rating scale is editable in Draft). |
| 2 | Transition the cycle Draft -> Active | 200; status Active; the rating scale is now locked. |
| 3 | Attempt to change the rating scale on the Active cycle — `PUT .../cycles/{id}` with a different scale | Rejected (400/409): the rating scale is locked once Active (BR-5); the stored scale is unchanged. |
| 4 | Inspect the UI | The rating-scale selector is disabled/read-only on an Active cycle, with an explanatory note. |
| 5 | Confirm related config | The weight ratio / 360 / calibration toggles follow the same lock semantics where the spec implies immutability after Active (assert at least that the rating scale specifically is locked). |

## 6. Postconditions
- The Active cycle's rating scale is immutable; participants are rated against a stable scale.
