# TC-PRF-008-08: Employee acknowledges PIP initiation; non-acknowledgement within 5 business days -> "Not Acknowledged" flag via Hangfire (BR-4)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-4: the employee must acknowledge PIP initiation (similar to review sign-off). On initiation the employee is notified and prompted to acknowledge; acknowledgement is recorded (with timestamp). If the employee does NOT acknowledge within 5 BUSINESS days, a Hangfire job sets a "Not Acknowledged" flag on the PIP and the PIP proceeds regardless (it is not blocked). Both branches (acknowledged / not acknowledged) are exercised.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PRF-008
- Business Rules: BR-4 (acknowledgement; 5-business-day non-ack flag)
- Functional Requirements: FR-3 (Hangfire jobs), FR-5 (immutable record of acknowledgement state)
- Non-Functional Requirements: NFR-2 (Hangfire job runs in tenant context)

## 3. Preconditions
- An Active PIP for Sam Lee exists in acme with acknowledgement pending (TC-PRF-008-01).
- A second Active PIP for Mara Voss exists in acme (to drive the non-ack branch).
- Hangfire is available; business-day calendar configured for acme.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| PIP A | Sam Lee | will acknowledge |
| PIP B | Mara Voss | will NOT acknowledge (timeout) |
| Ack window | 5 business days | BR-4 |
| Flag | "Not Acknowledged" | set by Hangfire on timeout |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As Sam, open the PIP-initiation notification and click "Acknowledge" | Acknowledgement recorded with actor + server timestamp (BR-4); PIP shows Acknowledged; the 5-business-day timer is satisfied (the timeout job becomes a no-op for this PIP). |
| 2 | Verify acknowledgement is immutable | The recorded acknowledgement cannot be altered/removed (FR-5). |
| 3 | For PIP B (Mara), do NOT acknowledge; advance the clock past 5 BUSINESS days (skipping weekends/holidays) | The Hangfire non-ack job fires and sets the PIP's "Not Acknowledged" flag (BR-4); the PIP PROCEEDS (status not blocked). |
| 4 | Confirm tenant context | The Hangfire ack-timeout job runs in acme tenant context and only flags acme PIPs (NFR-2, see TC-PRF-ISO-032). |
| 5 | Acknowledge PIP B AFTER the flag is set | If the implementation permits late acknowledgement, the flag history is retained (the "Not Acknowledged" event is not silently erased); asserted against whatever the implementation documents. |
| 6 | Confirm business-day math | The 5-day window counts BUSINESS days only -- weekends/holidays in the acme calendar do not count toward the timeout. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Sam's PIP is Acknowledged; Mara's PIP carries a "Not Acknowledged" flag and proceeds; both states recorded immutably in acme. No Tenant B data touched.
