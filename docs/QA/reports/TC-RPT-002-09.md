# TC-RPT-002-09: Manager Team-scope (direct reports via ReportsToEmployeeId) vs HR Officer full-tenant (AC-4, FR-8)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify permission-based data scoping: a Manager with `Reports.View.Team` sees only their DIRECT
REPORTS (employees whose `ReportsToEmployeeId` = the manager) in leave/attendance reports, while an
HR Officer with `Reports.View.All` sees the whole tenant. The SAME report type yields a strict
subset for the manager. Validates AC-4 and FR-8.

> PLATFORM NOTE / STORY MISMATCH: the permission catalog today exposes a single `Reports.View`
> (+ `Reports.Export`), not the `Reports.View.Team` / `Reports.View.All` split AC-4 / FR-8 require.
> Closing AC-4 needs scoped permission variants OR a manager direct-reports data filter keyed on
> `ReportsToEmployeeId` to be ADDED — permission-granularity gap flagged to the caller. This TC is the
> forward-looking acceptance test for that scope behaviour.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-RPT-002
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-4
- Functional Requirements: FR-7, FR-8
- Business Rules: BR-1

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant A active.
- Manager `mgr1` (Reports.View.Team) has exactly 4 direct reports (ReportsToEmployeeId = mgr1).
- Tenant A has 10 employees total (so 6 are NOT mgr1's reports).
- HR Officer `hr1` (Reports.View.All) exists.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| mgr1 direct reports | 4 | via ReportsToEmployeeId |
| tenant total | 10 | full population |
| report_type | leave_utilization | run by both personas |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As `mgr1`, generate Leave Utilization for the team | Only the 4 direct reports' leave data is included; the other 6 employees are absent |
| 2 | As `mgr1`, generate Attendance Summary | Same 4-employee scope; aggregates computed over direct reports only |
| 3 | As `mgr1`, attempt to filter by an employee who is NOT a direct report | That employee's data is not returned (out of team scope) |
| 4 | As `hr1`, generate the IDENTICAL Leave Utilization | All 10 employees included — full tenant |
| 5 | Diff mgr1's output vs hr1's output | mgr1's result is a strict SUBSET (the 4 reports) of hr1's 10 |
| 6 | Reassign one report away from mgr1 (change ReportsToEmployeeId); regenerate as mgr1 | That employee drops out of mgr1's report -> scope tracks the reporting line |

## 6. Postconditions
- Manager sees only direct reports; HR Officer sees full tenant.
