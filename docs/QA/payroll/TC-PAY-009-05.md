# TC-PAY-009-05: Payroll Variance Report -- month-over-month comparison highlights > 10% increase/decrease (BR-4)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify FR-1g / BR-4: the Payroll Variance Report compares a period against the prior period (month-over-month) for each employee and department, computes the variance, and highlights significant changes -- those whose increase OR decrease exceeds 10%. Changes at or below 10% are not flagged; changes above 10% are visually highlighted; the 10% threshold boundary is exercised exactly.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-009
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1
- Functional Requirements: FR-1g
- Business Rules: BR-4, BR-5
- Data Requirements: S7

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme"; Finalized April 2026 and May 2026 runs exist for comparison.
- HR user `hr1` with `Payroll.*.All`.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Emp E1 | Apr net 100,000 -> May net 100,000 | 0% (not flagged) |
| Emp E2 | Apr net 100,000 -> May net 110,000 | exactly +10% (boundary, not > 10%) |
| Emp E3 | Apr net 100,000 -> May net 115,000 | +15% (flagged increase) |
| Emp E4 | Apr net 100,000 -> May net 80,000 | -20% (flagged decrease) |
| Dept Sales | Apr 500,000 -> May 600,000 | +20% (flagged dept) |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As hr1, generate the Payroll Variance Report comparing May 2026 vs April 2026. | A per-employee + per-department comparison table renders with prior, current, delta, and % variance columns (FR-1g). |
| 2 | Inspect E1 (0%) and E2 (exactly +10%). | Neither is highlighted -- variance must exceed 10% to flag; the 10% boundary is NOT flagged (BR-4). |
| 3 | Inspect E3 (+15%) and E4 (-20%). | Both are highlighted as significant changes; direction (increase/decrease) is distinguishable (BR-4). |
| 4 | Inspect the Sales department row (+20%). | The department aggregate is highlighted as a significant change (BR-4). |
| 5 | Filter the report to "significant changes only". | Only E3, E4, and Sales appear; E1/E2 are excluded (FR-1g). |
| 6 | Compare against a period with no prior Finalized run. | The variance report indicates "no comparison period available"; no fabricated baseline (BR-1/BR-5 fiscal context). |

## 6. Postconditions
- The variance report flags only > 10% changes, respects the exact-10% boundary, and distinguishes increase vs decrease at employee and department level.
