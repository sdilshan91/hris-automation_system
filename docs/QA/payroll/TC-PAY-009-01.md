# TC-PAY-009-01: Payroll Summary Report -- period totals + department-wise breakdown table + bar chart (happy path)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-1 / FR-1a / FR-1c: HR navigates to Payroll Reports, selects "Payroll Summary Report" for May 2026, and the system displays a summary with total gross, total deductions, total statutory, total net, and employee count; a department-wise breakdown is rendered both as a table (Department, Employee Count, Total Basic, Total Allowances, Total Gross, Total Statutory, Total Other Deductions, Total Net) and a horizontal bar chart sorted by total cost descending. All figures are sourced from the Finalized May 2026 run's payslips for the tenant.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-009
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1
- Functional Requirements: FR-1a, FR-1c, FR-3, FR-5
- Non-Functional Requirements: NFR-6
- Business Rules: BR-1, BR-5
- Data Requirements: S7 (Payroll Summary Report Columns; pre-aggregated table)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" Active, subdomain `acme.yourhrm.com`, Payroll module enabled.
- A Finalized payroll run exists for May 2026 (US-PAY-003/008) with slips across >= 2 departments (e.g. Engineering, Sales).
- HR user `hr1` authenticated in acme with `Payroll.*.All` (or `Reports.*.All`).

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Period | May 2026 | Finalized run |
| Departments | Engineering (8 emps), Sales (5 emps) | breakdown rows |
| Expected totals | total_gross, total_statutory, total_other_deductions, total_net, employee_count=13 | summary card |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As hr1, open Payroll Reports; select "Payroll Summary Report"; set period = May 2026; Generate. | Summary card shows total gross, total deductions, total statutory, total net, and employee count (13) for the Finalized May run (AC-1, FR-1a). |
| 2 | Inspect the department-wise breakdown table. | One row per department (Engineering, Sales) with Department, Employee Count, Total Basic, Total Allowances, Total Gross, Total Statutory, Total Other Deductions, Total Net per S7 (FR-1c). |
| 3 | Confirm the column totals across department rows reconcile to the summary card. | sum(department Total Gross) == summary Total Gross; same for statutory/other-deductions/net; sum(Employee Count) == 13 (AC-1). |
| 4 | Inspect the department breakdown bar chart. | A horizontal bar chart renders, one bar per department, sorted by total cost descending; hover tooltips show exact values; chart renders from pre-aggregated data (FR-5, NFR-6). |
| 5 | Confirm the report scopes only to acme's Finalized May run. | No data from other tenants or from non-Finalized runs is included (BR-1). |
| 6 | Switch the period to a month with no Finalized run. | Empty-state message ("No finalized payroll for this period"); no totals fabricated (BR-1). |

## 6. Postconditions
- The Payroll Summary Report for acme May 2026 displays reconciled totals + department breakdown table + bar chart; no cross-tenant or non-Finalized data shown.
