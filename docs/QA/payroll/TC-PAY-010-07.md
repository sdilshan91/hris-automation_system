# TC-PAY-010-07: Unapproved overtime EXCLUDED from overtime earnings (negative, BR-4)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-4 / FR-4: overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's manager to be eligible for overtime earnings. Logged-but-unapproved OT hours are excluded from the overtime_amount; only the approved portion is paid. Mixed approved/unapproved OT pays only the approved hours.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-010
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-1, FR-4
- Business Rules: BR-4

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" Active; Payroll module enabled; OT component configured (multiplier 1.5x, base hourly 200).
- Attendance for the period FINALIZED. Employee logged 14 OT hours total: 10 manager-APPROVED + 4 UNAPPROVED.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Logged OT hours | 14 | 10 approved + 4 unapproved |
| Eligible (approved) OT | 10 | BR-4 |
| Excluded OT | 4 | unapproved |
| Expected overtime_amount | 10 * 200 * 1.5 = 3,000 | only approved paid |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Run payroll for the employee with 10 approved + 4 unapproved OT hours. | `overtime_hours = 10` (approved only); overtime_amount = 3,000; the 4 unapproved hours contribute 0 (BR-4). |
| 2 | Inspect the payslip / reconciliation. | The unapproved 4 hours appear in the attendance summary but are flagged not-eligible and excluded from pay. |
| 3 | Negative: all 14 hours unapproved. | overtime_amount = 0; no OT earning line added. |
| 4 | Approve the remaining 4 hours and re-run on a non-finalized run. | overtime_hours becomes 14; overtime_amount = 14 * 200 * 1.5 = 4,200 -- approval state drives eligibility. |
| 5 | Verify gross/net. | Gross/net include only the approved-OT amount at each step; totals reconcile. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Only manager-approved overtime is paid; unapproved OT is excluded from overtime earnings.
