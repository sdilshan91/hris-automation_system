# TC-NTF-003-07: Quiet Hours queues email at 23:00 (sent after 07:00); in-app stays real-time

## 1. Test Objective
Verify that with Quiet Hours 22:00-07:00 in the user's timezone, an email notification triggered at
23:00 (inside quiet hours) is QUEUED and delivered after quiet hours end (>= 07:00), while the
corresponding in-app notification is delivered in real time. Quiet Hours affects email timing only.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-NTF-003
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2 (channel delivery per preference)
- Functional Requirements: FR-9 (Quiet Hours), FR-6 (applied at dispatch time)
- Business Rules: BR-5 (Quiet Hours queues email, sends after end; in-app always real-time)

## 3. Preconditions
- Employee `emp1` (Tenant A) has Quiet Hours 22:00-07:00, timezone "Asia/Colombo", with In-App and
  Email both enabled for the test category (e.g. "Leave Updates").
- The email outbox/Hangfire worker is operational; clock/time can be controlled or fast-forwarded for the test.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| quiet_hours_start | 22:00 | |
| quiet_hours_end | 07:00 | |
| quiet_hours_timezone | Asia/Colombo | |
| trigger time | 23:00 local | inside quiet hours |
| category | Leave Updates | in-app + email enabled |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | At 23:00 (user's local time), trigger a leave-approval event for `emp1` | Event is raised; Dispatcher evaluates channels for the category |
| 2 | Observe in-app delivery | In-app notification is delivered to `emp1` in real time (BR-5: in-app not affected by quiet hours) |
| 3 | Inspect the email outbox/queue immediately | The email is QUEUED/scheduled (not sent), with a release time at/after 07:00 local (FR-9, BR-5) |
| 4 | Advance the clock to 06:59 local | Email is still NOT sent (boundary just before quiet hours end) |
| 5 | Advance the clock to 07:00 local and let the worker run | The queued email is now sent to `emp1`, rendered correctly |
| 6 | Trigger a second event at 12:00 local (outside quiet hours) | Email is sent immediately (no queuing); in-app also real-time |

## 6. Postconditions
- Email triggered inside quiet hours is delivered exactly once at/after 07:00; in-app delivered immediately; outside quiet hours email is immediate.
