# TC-ONB-006-05: Analytics — 10 interviews with varied reasons; reason pie distribution + average ratings per category correct and tenant-scoped

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-4, FR-4 and BR-4: with 10 exit interviews recorded for the tenant with varying "Reason for Leaving" selections and ratings, the "Exit Interview Summary" analytics view shows aggregated data: a reason-distribution pie chart whose proportions match the recorded reasons, average ratings per category (bar chart), and trends over time (line chart), all scoped strictly to the tenant's own data.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-ONB-006
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-4
- Functional Requirement: FR-4 (reason distribution, average ratings, trend analysis)
- Business Rule: BR-4 (analytics show only current-tenant data)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant `acme` has exactly 10 completed exit interviews with a known mix of reasons (e.g. 4x Compensation, 3x Career Growth, 2x Management, 1x Relocation) and known per-category ratings.
- A user authenticated in `acme` with permission to view exit interview analytics.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| total interviews | 10 | tenant acme |
| reasons | 4 Compensation / 3 Career Growth / 2 Management / 1 Relocation | drives pie |
| category ratings | known fixed set | drives averages |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Navigate to "Exit Interview Summary" for tenant acme | Analytics page loads with pie (reasons), bar (avg ratings/category), and line (trend) charts (AC-4, FR-4). |
| 2 | Inspect the reason pie distribution | Slices/percentages match 4/3/2/1 of 10 (40% / 30% / 20% / 10%) exactly (FR-4). |
| 3 | Inspect the average rating per category | Each category's displayed average equals the arithmetic mean of the recorded ratings for that category (FR-4). |
| 4 | Inspect the trend chart over a configurable time period | Interview counts are bucketed correctly by the chosen period (FR-4). |
| 5 | Confirm scope | All aggregates reflect only acme's 10 interviews; no other tenant's data contributes (BR-4). |

## 6. Postconditions
- Analytics render accurate, tenant-scoped aggregates with no cross-tenant contamination.
