# TC-ONB-003-09: Overdue detection — Hangfire job finds past-due task -> overdue notification outbox to employee/HR/manager

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-5, FR-6 and BR-4: a daily Hangfire scheduled job detects task instances past their due date and still incomplete, marks them "overdue", and writes overdue notification intent rows (outbox) addressed to the employee, the HR Officer, and the employee's manager. Real-time/email delivery is deferred to Notifications. The job sends overdue notifications once per day (BR-4).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-ONB-003
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-5
- Functional Requirements: FR-6
- Business Rules: BR-4
- Dependencies (deferred delivery): US-NTF-001 (in-app), US-NTF-002 (email)

> PLATFORM NOTE: AC-5/FR-6 describe overdue notifications delivered to employee, HR, and manager. Real delivery is owned by Notifications (US-NTF-001/002). This test asserts the onboarding side: the overdue status transition + outbox intent rows (employee + HR + manager) + Hangfire job execution. End-to-end SignalR/email receipt is deferred to the US-NTF test cases. BR-4 "once per day at tenant-configurable time (default 9:00 AM tenant tz)" — assert idempotency (no duplicate overdue notices on a same-day re-run).

## 3. Preconditions
- Employee "Alex Reed" in tenant `acme` with manager "Dana Lee" and HR Officer "Priya Nair".
- Task "Submit signed contract" (TK90) is "pending" with a due date of yesterday.
- Hangfire is configured and running (PostgreSQL storage).

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| overdue task | TK90, due = yesterday, pending | triggers overdue |
| recipients expected | Alex Reed, Priya Nair (HR), Dana Lee (Manager) | per FR-6 / AC-5 |
| job | onboarding overdue-detection (Hangfire daily) | BR-4 once/day |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Trigger the Hangfire overdue-detection job | Job runs against tenant acme; TK90 identified as past-due + incomplete (FR-6). |
| 2 | Inspect TK90 status | Transitioned to "overdue" (AC-5). |
| 3 | Inspect the notification outbox | Overdue notification intent rows exist for the employee (Alex), HR (Priya), and manager (Dana), all tenant-scoped to acme (FR-6, AC-5). |
| 4 | Re-run the job same day | No duplicate overdue notifications generated for TK90 (BR-4 once-per-day idempotency). |
| 5 | Confirm a non-overdue pending task is untouched | A pending task with a future due date is NOT marked overdue and gets no notification. |
| 6 | Confirm deferral boundary | No assertion on actual SignalR/email receipt — deferred to US-NTF-001/002. |

## 6. Postconditions
- TK90 marked "overdue"; tenant-scoped overdue notification intents queued for employee/HR/manager; same-day re-run is idempotent; future-dated tasks unaffected.
