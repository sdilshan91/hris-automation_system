# TC-PRF-010-14: History/trend -> two cycles of recommendations -> trend comparison correct (FR-7/BR-7)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify FR-7 / BR-7: the system maintains a history of all recommendations across cycles for trend analysis, retained indefinitely for compliance. With two completed cycles' recommendation history (FY24 + FY25), the trend comparison shows the correct cross-cycle figures (e.g. promotions, bonus pool, increment volume per cycle) in chronological order. Historical records are not deleted when a new cycle runs.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PRF-010
- Functional Requirements: FR-7 (history across cycles for trend analysis)
- Business Rules: BR-7 (history retained indefinitely)
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-4 (previous-cycle comparison feeds the trend)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme"; HR Officer Pat Ng (`Performance.Read.All`).
- FY24 recommendation history: 4 promotions, $30,000 bonus pool.
- FY25 recommendation history: 6 promotions, $48,000 bonus pool.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | FY24 | FY25 |
|-------|------|------|
| Promotions | 4 | 6 |
| Bonus pool | $30,000 | $48,000 |
| Order | earlier | later |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As Pat, open the recommendation history/trend view | Both FY24 and FY25 appear in chronological order with their per-cycle aggregates (FR-7). Only acme history. |
| 2 | Verify the trend series | Promotions trend 4 -> 6 (+2); bonus pool $30,000 -> $48,000 (+$18,000); the series matches the underlying recommendation history exactly (no recompute drift). |
| 3 | Confirm retention (BR-7) | FY24 recommendation records are fully retained after FY25 ran -- nothing was purged/overwritten by the new cycle; the FY24 details are still drillable. |
| 4 | Filter the trend by department (if supported) | The per-department trend (e.g. Engineering promotions FY24 vs FY25) is consistent with the per-cycle summaries. |
| 5 | Single-cycle boundary | A tenant with only one completed cycle shows that cycle alone (no spurious comparison); the trend handles the single-point case gracefully. |

## 6. Postconditions
- The cross-cycle recommendation trend is correct and chronological; historical records are retained indefinitely (BR-7). No data mutated. Tenant-scoped to acme.
