# TC-PRF-010-10: Downstream integration -> approved promotion -> Core HR update; bonus -> Payroll one-time earning; training -> Training enrollment (triggered on APPROVAL, not submission)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-6 and the Assumptions/Dependencies: when a recommendation is APPROVED (not at submission), it feeds the relevant downstream module -- an approved promotion updates the Core HR record (new grade/band + effective date), an approved bonus feeds Payroll as a one-time earning, and an approved training nomination creates a Training-module enrollment record. The downstream effect is asserted as an integration SEAM/event triggered on the approval transition; it must NOT fire on submission or on rejection.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PRF-010
- Business Rules: BR-6 (approved -> Core HR / Payroll / Training)
- Dependencies: Core HR (grade/band/comp), Payroll (one-time earnings), Training (enrollment)
- Assumptions/Constraints: downstream updates triggered AFTER approval, not at submission

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme"; the approval workflow from TC-PRF-010-04 is configured.
- An approved Promotion (Jordan -> G6, effective 2026-07-01), an approved Bonus (Emp E, $5,000), and an approved Training nomination (Emp F -> "Leadership 101") exist OR are driven to Approved in this test.
- A submitted-but-not-yet-approved recommendation and a rejected recommendation exist (negative controls).
- The Payroll one-time-earning seam and the Training enrollment seam are available (event/enqueue asserted; full processing conditional on those modules).

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Drive Jordan's Promotion to Approved (all approvers approve) | On the approval transition, a Core HR update event/seam is triggered: Jordan's grade -> G6 with effective date 2026-07-01 (asserted at the integration seam; the actual Core HR write/effective-dating is the Core HR module's responsibility). Tenant-scoped to acme. |
| 2 | Drive Emp E's Bonus to Approved | A Payroll one-time-earning event is enqueued for Emp E ($5,000) for the appropriate pay period; asserted at the Payroll seam (delivery conditional on the Payroll module). |
| 3 | Drive Emp F's Training nomination to Approved | A Training-module enrollment record/event is created for Emp F -> "Leadership 101" (asserted at the Training seam). |
| 4 | Inspect Emp G (submitted, NOT approved) | NO downstream Core HR/Payroll/Training effect fired -- the trigger is approval, not submission (Assumptions). |
| 5 | Inspect Emp H (rejected) | NO downstream effect fired -- a rejected recommendation never integrates. |
| 6 | Confirm tenant + idempotency | Each downstream event carries acme's tenant_id (server-derived); re-running the approval transition does not double-fire (idempotent seam). |

## 6. Postconditions
- Approved promotion/bonus/training recommendations each emit exactly one tenant-scoped downstream integration event (Core HR / Payroll / Training); submitted-but-unapproved and rejected recommendations emit none. Asserted at the integration seams. Tenant-scoped to acme.
