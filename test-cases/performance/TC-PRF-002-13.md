# TC-PRF-002-13: Submitted self-assessment is locked unless manager/HR reopens it (BR-3)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-3: once submitted, a self-assessment is locked and the employee cannot edit it. A manager (of that employee) or HR can explicitly reopen it, which returns it to an editable state so the employee can revise and re-submit; an employee cannot reopen their own.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PRF-002
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2 (prevents further edits unless reopened)
- Business Rules: BR-3

## 3. Preconditions
- acme cycle FY26-H1 self-assessment window open; Asha has a SUBMITTED self-assessment.
- Ravi is Asha's direct manager; an HR officer with `Performance` admin rights exists.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Submitted assessment | Asha / FY26-H1 | status "Self-Assessment Submitted" |
| Reopener (allowed) | Ravi (manager) / HR | may reopen |
| Reopener (denied) | Asha herself | may not reopen |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As Asha, open the submitted assessment and attempt to edit/re-submit | Read-only/locked; inputs disabled; a bypass edit returns 403/409 "submitted assessment is locked" (BR-3). |
| 2 | As Asha, attempt to reopen her own assessment | Denied 403 -- the employee cannot reopen their own submission. |
| 3 | As Ravi (manager) or HR, reopen Asha's assessment | Accepted; status returns to an editable state (e.g. `draft`/`reopened`); an audit/notification of the reopen is recorded (delivery CONDITIONAL on S25). |
| 4 | As Asha, after reopen, edit a rating/comment and re-submit | Edits now allowed; re-submit succeeds and status returns to "Self-Assessment Submitted"; the manager is re-notified. |
| 5 | Confirm tenant + ownership scoping | A manager/HR of another tenant cannot reopen acme's assessment; only Asha's own (or HR-permitted) record is affected. |

## 6. Postconditions
- A submitted assessment is immutable by the employee until a manager/HR reopens it; reopen restores editability and re-submission.
