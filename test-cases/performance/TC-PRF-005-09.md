# TC-PRF-005-09: Hangfire reviewer reminder fires to non-submitters only, with a deep link to the pending feedback form (AC-5/FR-8)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-5 / FR-8: as the feedback deadline approaches, a Hangfire job at the configured interval sends a reminder to each reviewer who has NOT yet submitted, with a direct deep link to their pending feedback form. Reviewers who already submitted are excluded. The job runs in the correct tenant context, is idempotent, and retries with backoff.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PRF-005
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-5
- Functional Requirements: FR-8
- Non-Functional Requirements: NFR-2 (tenant-scoped job)

## 3. Preconditions
- 360-enabled cycle "FY26 Annual Review" active for acme; Liam under review with 5 reviewers assigned.
- Reviewer reminder interval configured (e.g. 2 days before deadline) in the cycle/360 config.
- Of the 5 reviewers, 2 have submitted (Ava, Sara) and 3 have NOT (Maya, Ben, Tom).

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Submitted | Ava, Sara | excluded from reminder |
| Not submitted | Maya, Ben, Tom | should be reminded |
| Reminder trigger | deadline - 2 days | FR-8 interval |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Advance time / trigger the Hangfire reminder job for FY26 360 | The job runs under the acme tenant context (NFR-2). |
| 2 | Inspect the reminders enqueued/sent | Reminders go ONLY to Maya, Ben, Tom (non-submitters); Ava and Sara receive nothing (AC-5/FR-8). |
| 3 | Inspect a reminder payload | Each reminder contains a direct deep link to that reviewer's pending 360 feedback form (e.g. `https://acme.yourhrm.com/performance/360/feedback/{assignmentId}`); in-app + email per the notification template (delivery CONDITIONAL on Notification System S25 — assert the enqueue). |
| 4 | Have Ben submit, then re-run the job | Ben is now excluded; only Maya + Tom are reminded — the non-submitter set is recomputed each run. |
| 5 | Force a transient delivery failure | The job retries with Polly exponential backoff and is idempotent — a reviewer is not double-reminded within a single scheduled run. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Only non-submitting reviewers are reminded, each with a working deep link; the job is tenant-scoped, idempotent, and retried on failure.
