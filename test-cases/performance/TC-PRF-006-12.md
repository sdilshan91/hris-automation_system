# TC-PRF-006-12: HR resolves a disputed review -- amend (reopen for re-sign) or confirm (finalize) (BR-4, FR-5)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-4 and FR-5 resolution paths: a review in "Disputed" status is resolved ONLY by HR, by either (a) AMENDING the review -- editing ratings/notes and re-requesting employee sign-off, returning the review to "Pending Employee Sign-Off" so the employee can re-review and sign; or (b) CONFIRMING the review -- upholding it as-is and moving it to a terminal resolved/Signed Off state per the documented rule. Verify a `.Team`-only manager cannot resolve a dispute, and the resolution action is audited (FR-7) and notifies the relevant parties.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PRF-006
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-3 (dispute lifecycle)
- Functional Requirements: FR-5 (escalate to HR for resolution), FR-7 (audit)
- Business Rules: BR-4 (Disputed until HR resolves by amend or confirm)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme", Performance module enabled.
- Liam Carter's review is in "Disputed" status with recorded dispute comments (from TC-PRF-006-04).
- HR Officer "Nadia Khan" (`Performance.Review.All`), manager "Maya Shah" (`.Team` only) exist in acme.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Review status | Disputed | BR-4 |
| Resolution: amend | edit + re-request sign-off | FR-5 |
| Resolution: confirm | uphold as-is | FR-5 |
| Unauthorized resolver | Maya (.Team) | blocked |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As Maya (`.Team` only), attempt to resolve the dispute (`POST .../reviews/{reviewId}/resolve-dispute`) | 403 -- only HR `Performance.Review.All` may resolve a dispute (BR-4/FR-5). |
| 2 | As Nadia (HR), choose AMEND: edit the disputed rating/notes and re-request employee sign-off | The review is amended; status returns to "Pending Employee Sign-Off"; the employee is re-notified to review and sign the amended record (FR-5); the prior dispute comments are retained for the audit trail. |
| 3 | As Liam, re-review the amended notes and "Acknowledge & Sign" | Sign-off succeeds; status -> "Signed Off"; review locked (BR-5); a new employee signature (name+timestamp+IP) is appended (NFR-3 append-only). |
| 4 | Set up a second disputed review; as Nadia choose CONFIRM (uphold as-is) | The review is confirmed per the documented rule (e.g. moves to a terminal resolved / Signed-Off-with-dispute-noted state); the dispute and resolution are both retained; status is no longer "Disputed". |
| 5 | Confirm audit + notifications | Both resolution actions (amend, confirm) are audit-logged with userId=Nadia + timestamp + IP (FR-7) and notify the relevant parties (CONDITIONAL on S25). |

## 6. Postconditions
- The disputed review is resolved only by HR via amend (reopened for re-sign) or confirm (finalized); a manager cannot resolve; resolutions are audited.
