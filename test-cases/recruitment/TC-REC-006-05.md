# TC-REC-006-05: Anti-bias -- an interviewer cannot view other interviewers' scorecards for the same interview until they submit their own

## 1. Test Objective
Verify FR-6 / BR-5: when two interviewers (empA, empB) are assigned to the same interview, empB CANNOT see empA's scorecard (scores, comments, recommendation) until empB has submitted their own. The hide must be enforced server-side (the API must not return empA's card to empB before empB submits), not merely a UI overlay. After empB submits, empA's card becomes visible to empB. This is the bias-prevention control; the recruiter is exempt (covered in TC-REC-006-04 step 7).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-006
- Functional Requirements: FR-6
- Business Rules: BR-5
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-3 (multi-interviewer context)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" active; interview {acme_interviewId} has assigned interviewers empA and empB.
- empA has ALREADY submitted a scorecard (4/5/3/4, Hire); empB has NOT yet submitted.
- empA and empB authenticate separately.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Interview | {acme_interviewId} | empA submitted, empB pending |
| empA card | scores + comments + Hire | Must be hidden from empB pre-submit |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As empB (not yet submitted), `GET` the scorecards for {acme_interviewId} | empA's card is NOT returned in the payload (no scores, comments, or recommendation); response indicates "submit your own first" (server-side hide, not just a UI overlay) (FR-6, BR-5). |
| 2 | As empB, attempt to fetch empA's card directly by id `GET /scorecards/{empA_scorecardId}` | 403/404; empB cannot read another interviewer's card on a shared interview before submitting. |
| 3 | As empB, submit a complete scorecard for {acme_interviewId} | 201; empB's card saved. |
| 4 | As empB, re-`GET` the scorecards for {acme_interviewId} | empA's card is NOW visible (scores, comments, recommendation) -- the anti-bias lock lifts after empB's own submission (BR-5). |
| 5 | Verify empB cannot bypass by editing | empB cannot create a placeholder/empty card to unlock viewing then edit it to game the order -- a submission must be a VALID complete card (recommendation mandatory; see TC-REC-006-07) to count as "submitted". |
| 6 | Verify the recruiter is unaffected | A Read.All recruiter sees empA's card at all times regardless of empB's state (recruiter exemption -- TC-REC-006-04). |

## 6. Postconditions
- empB could not see empA's card until empB submitted; after submission both cards are mutually visible to the assigned interviewers.
