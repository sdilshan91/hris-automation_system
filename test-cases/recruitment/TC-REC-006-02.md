# TC-REC-006-02: Interview transitions to Completed ONLY when ALL assigned interviewers have submitted

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-1 / FR-4: the interview status flips to `Completed` only after EVERY assigned interviewer has submitted a scorecard -- not on the first submission. With two assigned interviewers (empA, empB), the interview must remain `Scheduled` after empA submits and transition to `Completed` only when empB also submits. This is the completion gate that TC-REC-006-01 deliberately did not exercise (single-interviewer case).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-006
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1
- Functional Requirements: FR-4
- Business Rules: BR-1 (each submits their own)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" active; interview {acme_interviewId} (Scheduled) has TWO assigned interviewers: empA and empB.
- Both empA and empB are authenticated separately.
- 4 configured criteria (defaults).

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Interview | {acme_interviewId} | Two interviewers: empA, empB |
| empA scores | 4/4/4/4 | recommendation=Hire |
| empB scores | 3/3/3/3 | recommendation=No Hire |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As empA, submit a complete scorecard for {acme_interviewId} | 201; empA scorecard saved (average 4.0). |
| 2 | Re-read the interview status | Status is STILL `Scheduled` -- NOT yet Completed, because empB has not submitted (FR-4). |
| 3 | Verify the recruiter sees partial progress | The applicant detail panel shows 1 of 2 scorecards submitted (pending empB); aggregate is computed over submitted scorecards only (see TC-REC-006-03). |
| 4 | As empB, submit a complete scorecard for {acme_interviewId} | 201; empB scorecard saved (average 3.0). |
| 5 | Re-read the interview status | Status is NOW `Completed` -- all assigned interviewers (empA + empB) have submitted (FR-4). |
| 6 | Negative: add a THIRD assignment (empC) to a fresh interview after one submission, then check status | A newly added pending interviewer keeps the interview out of Completed until they too submit (completion is evaluated against the CURRENT assigned set, not a snapshot). |

## 6. Postconditions
- Interview {acme_interviewId} is `Completed` only after both empA and empB submitted; status remained `Scheduled` while any assigned interviewer was outstanding.
