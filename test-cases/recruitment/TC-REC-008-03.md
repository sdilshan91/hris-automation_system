# TC-REC-008-03: Applicant accepts offer on the portal -> offer status Accepted + applicant advances to "Hired" (happy path)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-3 / FR-5 / BR-2: when the applicant has an active `Sent` offer (US-REC-007), the portal shows the offer details (position, salary, start date, expiry date) and the offer letter PDF download, with "Accept" and "Decline" buttons. Clicking "Accept" presents a confirmation dialog; on confirm the system records the response, sets the offer status to `Accepted`, and advances the applicant to the "Hired" pipeline stage (mirrors US-REC-007's accept flow + REC-004 BR-3 Hired terminal). The action is one-time and cannot be changed by the applicant afterwards (BR-2). This is the portal-driven accept path; the recruiter-side accept is TC-REC-007-03.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-008
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-3
- Functional Requirements: FR-5 (offer details + PDF + Accept/Decline + confirm dialog), FR-4 (PDF download via signed URL)
- Business Rules: BR-2 (accept/decline one-time), BR-1 (read-only otherwise)
- Cross-story: US-REC-007 (offer), US-REC-004 BR-3 (Hired advancement)

## 3. Preconditions
- Applicant {acme_applicantId} on {acme_vacancyId} has an active offer {acme_offerId} in status `Sent` (US-REC-007), not expired.
- The offer PDF exists at acme's tenant-scoped path; a valid magic link for the applicant is available.

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Open the portal and view the offer section | A highlighted offer card shows position, salary, start date, expiry date, a PDF download button, and prominent "Accept" / "Decline" buttons (AC-3, FR-5). |
| 2 | Click "Download" on the offer PDF | The offer letter PDF downloads via a signed, short-lived blob URL (no raw blob URL exposed) (FR-4). |
| 3 | Click "Accept" | A confirmation modal appears asking the applicant to confirm acceptance before submission (FR-5). |
| 4 | Confirm acceptance | The response is recorded; offer {acme_offerId} status becomes `Accepted`; `responded_at` set; the applicant advances to the "Hired" pipeline stage; the expiry Hangfire job is cancelled (AC-3, BR-2). |
| 5 | Re-load the portal | The offer now shows as Accepted; Accept/Decline buttons are disabled/removed -- the response cannot be changed by the applicant (BR-2). |
| 6 | Verify stage | The application's step indicator shows "Hired" as completed/current. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Offer {acme_offerId} = Accepted; {acme_applicantId} = Hired; expiry job cancelled; response is immutable from the portal side.
