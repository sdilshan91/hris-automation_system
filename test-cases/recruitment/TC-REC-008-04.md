# TC-REC-008-04: Applicant declines offer on the portal -> offer status Declined; applicant remains in "Offer" stage (alternative path)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify the alternative offer-response path (AC-3, FR-5, BR-2): clicking "Decline" on an active `Sent` offer shows a confirmation dialog; on confirm the offer status becomes `Declined`, the applicant does NOT advance to Hired (remains in "Offer" stage), and the decline is a one-time action the applicant cannot reverse (BR-2). Mirrors US-REC-007's decline behaviour (TC-REC-007-04) but driven from the candidate portal.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-008
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-3
- Functional Requirements: FR-5 (Accept/Decline + confirm dialog)
- Business Rules: BR-2 (one-time action; recruiter may override)
- Cross-story: US-REC-007 (TC-REC-007-04 decline)

## 3. Preconditions
- Applicant {acme_applicantId} has an active offer {acme_offerId} in status `Sent`, not expired; valid magic link available.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Offer | {acme_offerId} | Sent |
| Action | Decline | one-time |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Open the portal, view the offer, click "Decline" | A confirmation modal appears (FR-5). |
| 2 | Confirm the decline | Offer {acme_offerId} status becomes `Declined`; `responded_at` set; the applicant REMAINS in the "Offer" stage (not advanced to Hired); the expiry job is cancelled (AC-3). |
| 3 | Re-load the portal | The offer shows Declined; Accept/Decline buttons are disabled/removed -- the response cannot be changed by the applicant (BR-2). |
| 4 | Verify the recruiter override seam | The recruiter side may still override the response (BR-2); this is asserted in the REC-007 recruiter flow, not from the portal. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Offer {acme_offerId} = Declined; applicant stays in "Offer" stage; response immutable from the portal side.
