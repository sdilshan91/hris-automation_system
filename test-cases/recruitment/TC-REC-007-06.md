# TC-REC-007-06: Withdraw offer before acceptance -> status Withdrawn, applicant notified, offer cannot be re-sent (FR-8, BR-7)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify offer withdrawal (FR-8, FR-6, BR-7): a recruiter with `Recruitment.Offer.All` withdraws an offer at any point BEFORE acceptance (from `Draft` or `Sent`). On withdrawal the system (a) sets offer status -> `Withdrawn`, (b) sends a notification to the applicant (if the offer was already Sent) and cancels the pending expiry/reminder Hangfire jobs, and (c) makes the withdrawn offer non-re-sendable -- a "Send" on a Withdrawn offer is rejected; the recruiter must generate a NEW offer (BR-7). Withdrawal after acceptance is NOT allowed (FR-8 says "before acceptance").

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-007
- Functional Requirements: FR-8 (withdrawal), FR-6 (Withdrawn status), FR-7 (job cancellation)
- Business Rules: BR-7 (withdrawn cannot be re-sent; new offer required)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" active; recruiter `recruiter@acme` with `Recruitment.Offer.All`.
- A `Sent` offer {acme_offerId} for {acme_applicantId} (and, for the negative arm, a separately Accepted offer {acme_acceptedOfferId}).
- Notification System (S25) + Hangfire wired or stubbed.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Sent offer | {acme_offerId} | withdrawable |
| Accepted offer | {acme_acceptedOfferId} | NOT withdrawable |
| Applicant email | applicant@example.com | withdrawal notification recipient |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As `recruiter@acme`, `POST /api/v1/recruitment/offers/{acme_offerId}/withdraw` | 200; offer status -> `Withdrawn` (FR-6, FR-8). |
| 2 | Verify applicant notification | Because the offer was Sent, a withdrawal notification is queued to applicant@example.com via Hangfire/outbox (or log stub); a Draft (never-sent) offer withdrawal sends no applicant email (FR-8). |
| 3 | Verify expiry jobs cancelled | The pending reminder + expiry auto-status jobs for {acme_offerId} are cancelled/no-op. |
| 4 | Attempt to re-send the withdrawn offer (`/offers/{acme_offerId}/send`) | Rejected (409/422); a Withdrawn offer cannot be re-sent (BR-7). |
| 5 | Verify a new offer is allowed | The recruiter can generate a NEW offer (new row, version increments) for the same applicant/vacancy (BR-7; see TC-REC-007-08). |
| 6 | Negative: withdraw an already-Accepted offer | `POST .../{acme_acceptedOfferId}/withdraw` is rejected; withdrawal is only allowed BEFORE acceptance (FR-8). |

## 6. Postconditions
- Offer {acme_offerId} status=`Withdrawn`, applicant notified (if was Sent), jobs cancelled, cannot be re-sent; the Accepted offer was not withdrawn.
