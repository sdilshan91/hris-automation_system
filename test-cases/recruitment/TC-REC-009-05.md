# TC-REC-009-05: Time-to-hire trend (line chart) shows average time-to-hire over the period with correct weekly/monthly data points (FR-4)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify FR-4 / BR-1: the time-to-hire trend line chart plots average time-to-hire over the selected period at weekly or monthly granularity (chosen by range length), where each data point is the mean of (hired_at - applied_at) in calendar days for applicants hired in that bucket. Asserts bucketed averages against a seeded dataset, that empty buckets render as gaps/zero consistently, and that hover tooltips show the exact bucket value.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-009
- Functional Requirements: FR-4 (time-to-hire trend line chart)
- Business Rules: BR-1 (time-to-hire = applied_at -> Hired in calendar days)
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2 (honours date filter granularity)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" seeded with hires across distinct weeks/months with known applied/hired dates, e.g. Week 1: 2 hires averaging 20 days; Week 2: 0 hires; Week 3: 3 hires averaging 30 days.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Week 1 | 2 hires, avg 20 days | data point = 20 |
| Week 2 | 0 hires | gap / 0 / no point |
| Week 3 | 3 hires, avg 30 days | data point = 30 |
| Range -> 1 year | monthly buckets | granularity switches to monthly |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | View the time-to-hire trend chart for a weekly-granularity period | Line chart renders weekly data points (FR-4). |
| 2 | Inspect the Week 1 point | Value = 20 days (mean of 2 hires per BR-1). |
| 3 | Inspect Week 2 | Rendered as a gap or 0 consistently (no hires); no NaN/error. |
| 4 | Inspect Week 3 | Value = 30 days. |
| 5 | Switch the date filter to "last year" | Granularity switches to monthly buckets; points recompute as monthly averages. |
| 6 | Hover a data point | Tooltip shows the bucket label and exact average-days value. |

## 6. Postconditions
- No data mutated; trend buckets and averages match BR-1 over the selected granularity.
