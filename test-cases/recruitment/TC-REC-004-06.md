# TC-REC-004-06: Reactivation -- a Rejected applicant cannot advance until moved back to an active stage by a Manage user (BR-2)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-2: "Rejected" behaves as a blocking terminal-until-reactivated state. A rejected applicant cannot be advanced to any forward stage directly; they must first be REACTIVATED (moved back to an active stage) by a user with `Recruitment.Manage.All`, with a reason. After reactivation, normal forward transitions resume. Each step is recorded in `applicant_stage_history`.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-004
- Business Rules: BR-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-3 (rejection), FR-5 (Manage-only backward/reactivation)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme".
- Applicant "Rae Okafor" at "Rejected" (rejected from Screening, reason=NotQualified).
- User1 = Manage; User2 = Read only.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Applicant | Rae Okafor ({acme_applicantId}) | Rejected |
| Reactivate target | Screening (active stage) | Chosen by Manage |
| Reactivation reason | "Reconsidered after team feedback" | Required |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As User1, attempt to move Rejected -> Interview directly (forward) | Blocked: a rejected applicant cannot advance without reactivation (BR-2); no change. |
| 2 | As User2 (Read only), attempt to reactivate Rejected -> Screening | 403; reactivation requires `Recruitment.Manage.All`. |
| 3 | As User1, reactivate Rejected -> Screening with a reason | 200; stage=Screening; history row from=Rejected, to=Screening, notes=reason, changed_by=User1. |
| 4 | As User1, now move Screening -> Interview (forward) | 200; normal forward transition resumes; history row from=Screening, to=Interview. |
| 5 | Verify timeline integrity | The timeline shows the full arc: ...->Rejected->Screening (reactivation)->Interview, each with reason/user/timestamp; no transition was lost or silently overwritten. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Rae Okafor is active again at "Interview"; the rejection, reactivation, and subsequent advance are all recorded.
