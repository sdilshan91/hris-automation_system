# TC-REC-009-08: Department and vacancy drill-down filters scope all dashboard widgets (FR-7)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify FR-7: applying a department filter and/or a specific-vacancy filter re-scopes every dashboard widget (KPI cards, funnel, source effectiveness, time-to-hire trend, vacancy status summary, recent activity) to that department/vacancy. Asserts that combining department + vacancy + date filters AND-combines correctly, that clearing a filter restores the broader scope, and that selecting a vacancy not in the chosen department behaves consistently (either disallowed or yields its own data). This is the recruiter/HR-Officer drill-down (distinct from the role-based department restriction in TC-REC-009-09).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-009
- Functional Requirements: FR-7 (department + vacancy drill-down), FR-6 (combines with date range)
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1, AC-2

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" with two departments (Engineering, Sales), each with multiple vacancies and applicants, plus known per-department and per-vacancy counts so the filtered widgets are predictable.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Department Engineering | 3 vacancies, 60 applicants | drill-down scope A |
| Department Sales | 2 vacancies, 40 applicants | drill-down scope B |
| Vacancy "Senior Engineer" | 25 applicants, 1 hire | single-vacancy scope |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Apply the Department = Engineering filter | All widgets re-scope: Total Applicants = 60, funnel/source/trend/status/activity reflect only Engineering (FR-7). |
| 2 | Add the Vacancy = "Senior Engineer" filter | Widgets narrow further to that vacancy: 25 applicants, funnel/source for that vacancy only (FR-7 AND FR-6 combine). |
| 3 | Add a date range filter | All three filters AND-combine; counts reflect Engineering + Senior Engineer + period. |
| 4 | Clear the vacancy filter | Scope widens back to Department = Engineering (60 applicants). |
| 5 | Switch Department to Sales | Widgets re-scope to Sales (40 applicants); no Engineering data leaks into the view. |
| 6 | Clear all drill-down filters | Dashboard restores the full-tenant view. |

## 6. Postconditions
- No data mutated; drill-down filters consistently and additively scoped every widget; clearing restored broader scope.
