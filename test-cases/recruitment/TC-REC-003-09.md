# TC-REC-003-09: "Hired" is a terminal stage and triggers the convert-to-employee workflow seam (negative + integration seam)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-6: the "Hired" stage is terminal -- once an applicant is moved to Hired, no further forward transition is permitted, and the move triggers the convert-to-employee workflow (US-REC-010). This test asserts the terminal behaviour and the trigger seam; the full convert-to-employee flow is owned by US-REC-010.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-003
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Business Rules: BR-6, BR-5
- Functional Requirements: FR-3
- Dependencies: US-REC-010 (convert-to-employee workflow)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" is active; a recruiter with `Recruitment.Manage.All` is authenticated.
- Applicant "Robin Diaz" exists at stage "Offer".

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Applicant | Robin Diaz | At "Offer" |
| To stage | Hired | Terminal (BR-6) |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As `manager@acme`, move Robin Diaz Offer -> Hired | 200/204; applicant moves to "Hired"; a `applicant_stage_history` row is recorded (BR-5). |
| 2 | Verify the convert-to-employee trigger | Moving to "Hired" emits/queues the convert-to-employee workflow signal for US-REC-010 (the integration seam fires). NOTE: the full employee-record creation is verified by US-REC-010; here only the trigger is asserted. |
| 3 | Attempt to move Robin Diaz forward out of "Hired" (e.g. to any later/other stage) | Rejected: "Hired" is terminal; no further forward transition is allowed (BR-6). Stage stays "Hired". |
| 4 | Attempt a backward move from "Hired" by `manager@acme` with a reason | Behaviour follows BR-4/BR-6 policy: if the platform forbids leaving the terminal Hired stage, the move is rejected; otherwise only Manage + reason is honoured. Assert the implemented policy and that any change is recorded in history. |
| 5 | Verify board counts | Offer decremented, Hired incremented; total unchanged after step 1. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Robin Diaz is at "Hired"; the convert-to-employee seam fired; no unauthorized forward transition out of the terminal stage occurred.
