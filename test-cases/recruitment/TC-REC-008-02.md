# TC-REC-008-02: Upcoming interview details displayed on the portal (date, time, type, location/video link, interviewer name(s))

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-2 / FR-3: when the applicant has an upcoming interview scheduled (US-REC-005), the candidate portal displays the interview details for their application: date, time, duration, type (in-person/video/phone), location (with map link) or video link (with "Join Meeting"), and interviewer name(s). Only the SANITIZED, applicant-facing fields are shown -- interviewer names are exposed (per story #10 assumptions), but interviewer comments, scorecards, and internal notes are NOT (NFR-5, see TC-REC-008-08).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-008
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-3 (interview details)
- Non-Functional Requirements: NFR-5 (no internal data leak -- only interviewer names, not comments)
- Business Rules: BR-3 (sanitized view)

## 3. Preconditions
- Applicant {acme_applicantId} has a `Scheduled` interview (US-REC-005) in the future: a video round with interviewers empA + empB.
- A second interview record exists that is `Completed` with submitted scorecards (to confirm scorecard/comments are NOT surfaced).
- A valid magic link for the applicant is available.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Interview (upcoming) | {acme_interviewId} | Scheduled, type=Video, +3 days, 45 min |
| Video link | https://meet.example.com/abc | -> "Join Meeting" |
| Interviewers | empA "Alice", empB "Bob" | names shown; comments NOT |
| Interview (past) | {acme_interviewId2} | Completed, has scorecards (must not surface) |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Open the portal via the magic link and view the application | The interview section renders a card with a calendar icon and the date/time prominently displayed (FR-3). |
| 2 | Inspect interview fields | Date, start time, duration (45 min), type badge "Video", and interviewer names "Alice", "Bob" are shown (AC-2, FR-3). |
| 3 | Inspect the join control | A "Join Meeting" button links to the video link; for an in-person interview a location with a map link would be shown instead (type-conditional) (FR-3). |
| 4 | Verify no internal data leaks from the completed round | The `Completed` interview's scorecard ratings, interviewer comments, and recommendation are NOT displayed anywhere on the portal (NFR-5, BR-3). |
| 5 | Verify scope | Only interviews tied to {acme_applicantId}'s own application(s) within acme are shown. |

## 6. Postconditions
- No data is mutated; applicant viewed sanitized upcoming-interview details for their own application.
