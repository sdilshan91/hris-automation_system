# TC-REC-007-11: Approval workflow (if configured) blocks send until approved (BR-5, FR-10) -- CONDITIONAL on Approval Workflow Engine (S34)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify the approval gate (BR-5, FR-10): when the tenant is configured to require offer approval (e.g. manager or HR-head approval), an offer CANNOT be sent until the approval workflow completes. The system blocks `send` on a pending-approval offer, surfaces the approval state, and only permits send once approval is granted (and re-blocks/withholds send if approval is rejected). When the tenant does NOT require approval, send proceeds directly (TC-REC-007-02).

CONDITIONAL: BR-5/FR-10 depend on the Approval Workflow Engine (S34), which is DEFERRED. If S34 is not yet wired in this increment, this case asserts the SEND-GATE contract against a stub: an offer flagged "requires approval" is held in a pending-approval state and `send` is rejected until the approval flag is satisfied. It MUST NOT be weakened to "send always allowed". When S34 lands, extend Steps 3-5 to drive the real approval workflow.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-REC-007
- Business Rules: BR-5 (approval blocks send)
- Functional Requirements: FR-10 (integrate with approval workflow engine S34)
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2 (send is gated)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" configured to REQUIRE offer approval; recruiter `recruiter@acme` (Offer.All); an approver `approver@acme`.
- A `Draft` offer {acme_offerId} for {acme_applicantId}.
- Approval Workflow Engine (S34) wired, OR a stub exposing the requires-approval flag + approve/reject actions (see Conditional note).

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As `recruiter@acme`, attempt to `send` {acme_offerId} while approval is pending | Rejected (409/422); the offer is NOT emailed, status does NOT become Sent; the response indicates approval is required/pending (BR-5, FR-10). |
| 2 | Verify approval state surfaced | The offer shows a pending-approval state to the recruiter; the approver is notified/has a pending task (CONDITIONAL on S34/notifications). |
| 3 | As `approver@acme`, APPROVE the offer (real S34 action or stub) | Approval recorded; the offer becomes send-eligible. |
| 4 | As `recruiter@acme`, send the now-approved offer | Send succeeds (status Sent, email + PDF, expiry jobs) per TC-REC-007-02. |
| 5 | Negative: approver REJECTS approval | Send remains blocked; the offer cannot be sent until re-submitted/approved; no email goes out. |
| 6 | Tenant WITHOUT approval policy | For a tenant not requiring approval, send proceeds directly with no approval step (TC-REC-007-02). |

## 6. Postconditions
- With approval required, send is blocked until approved and re-blocked on rejection; without the policy, send proceeds directly.
