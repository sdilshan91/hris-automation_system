# TC-PAY-005-07: Employee with full employment history (incl. department transfers) sees all their Finalized payslips; terminated employee access follows the tenant post-termination policy (boundary)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-2 / BR-3: an employee can view their entire employment history within the tenant -- including payslips from months before a department transfer -- with each historical payslip retaining its point-in-time department/designation. Separately, when an employee is terminated, their payslip access is governed by the tenant's configurable post-termination access policy (immediate revoke / 30 days / permanent read-only).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-005
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1, AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-2, FR-8
- Business Rules: BR-2, BR-3
- Note: BR-3 policy enforcement is CONDITIONAL on the tenant post-termination access-policy surface; if that config does not yet exist, the default observed behaviour (e.g. login-revoked-on-termination) is asserted and the policy-driven branch is noted as deferred.

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme". Employee EMP-010 with `Payroll.Read.Self`, employed Jan 2025 onward, transferred from "Sales" to "Marketing" in Sep 2025 -- Finalized payslips exist on both sides of the transfer.
- A second employee EMP-011 is Terminated effective 31 May 2026, with Finalized payslips through May 2026.
- Tenant post-termination access policy configurable: immediate-revoke | 30-days | permanent-read-only.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| EMP-010 transfer | Sales (pre-Sep 2025) -> Marketing (post) | BR-2 |
| Pre-transfer slip | Mar 2025, department "Sales" | point-in-time |
| Post-transfer slip | Dec 2025, department "Marketing" | point-in-time |
| EMP-011 | Terminated 31 May 2026 | BR-3 |
| Policies | revoke / 30-day / permanent | BR-3 |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As EMP-010, open My Payslips. | All Finalized payslips across the full employment history are listed (both pre- and post-transfer months), most-recent-first (BR-2). |
| 2 | Open the Mar 2025 (pre-transfer) detail. | Header shows department "Sales" (the point-in-time department at that pay period), not the current "Marketing" (BR-2). |
| 3 | Open the Dec 2025 (post-transfer) detail. | Header shows department "Marketing"; both historical slips remain accessible. |
| 4 | Set tenant policy = immediate-revoke; terminate EMP-011; attempt EMP-011 login/list. | EMP-011 cannot access My Payslips (access revoked on termination, BR-3). |
| 5 | Set tenant policy = 30-days; check EMP-011 access within 30 days of termination, then after 30 days. | Within window: read-only list + download work; after 30 days: access denied (BR-3). |
| 6 | Set tenant policy = permanent-read-only; check EMP-011 access. | EMP-011 retains read-only access to their Finalized payslips indefinitely; no edit/delete (BR-3 + BR-4). |

## 6. Postconditions
- Full-history access works with correct point-in-time department; terminated-employee access matches the active tenant policy.
