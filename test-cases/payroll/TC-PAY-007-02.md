# TC-PAY-007-02: Deduction adjustment subtracts from net salary in the payslip (negative-direction earning)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-2 / FR-1 / FR-3 / BR-3: a one-time Deduction adjustment for an employee, picked up by the payroll run for its applicable period, is subtracted from the employee's net salary and shown as a deduction line on the payslip; the deduction reduces net (not gross earnings) and the math reconciles.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-007
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-1, FR-3
- Business Rules: BR-3 (deduction reduces net; cannot go negative)
- Data Requirements: S7 (payroll_adjustment: adjustment_type=Deduction)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme"; user with `Payroll.*.All`; Employee X active structure; gross/net materially larger than the deduction (no negative-net concern in this case).
- No Finalized run yet for the applicable period.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Employee X net (pre-adjustment) | 80,000 | for the period |
| Deduction | type=Deduction, amount=5000.00, period=2026-06, description "Salary advance recovery" | AC-2 |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Create a Deduction adjustment for Employee X amount=5000, period=June 2026; Save. | `payroll_adjustment` persists type=Deduction, amount=5000.00, status=Pending (FR-1). |
| 2 | Execute the June 2026 payroll run. | The run includes the deduction as a deduction line item for Employee X (FR-3, BR-3). |
| 3 | Open Employee X's June payslip. | A "Salary advance recovery" deduction line of 5,000 appears; gross/earnings unchanged; net = pre-adjustment net (80,000) minus 5,000 = 75,000 (AC-2, BR-3). |
| 4 | Verify reconciliation: sum(earnings) - sum(deductions incl. adjustment) == net. | Penny reconciliation holds; the adjustment is on the deductions side of the ledger, not the earnings side (BR-3). |
| 5 | Confirm the deduction adjustment is NOT treated as taxable income / does not increase gross. | Gross unchanged; adjustment affects net only (BR-3). |

## 6. Postconditions
- Employee X's June payslip net is reduced by the deduction; the adjustment is Applied after finalization.
