# TC-PAY-003-05: LOP (Loss of Pay) computed correctly -- 3 absent days without approved leave in a 22-working-day month (BR-2)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-2 and FR-5b: LOP is calculated as `daily_rate * LOP_days` where `daily_rate = monthly_basic / working_days_in_month` and `LOP_days = absent_days_without_approved_leave`. Absences that are covered by approved leave do NOT count toward LOP. The computed LOP reduces net salary and is reflected in `lop_days` / `paid_days` on the slip and in a deduction (or reduced earning) detail line with a `calculation_basis`.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-003
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-5 (b. LOP calculation)
- Business Rules: BR-2
- Data Requirements: S7 (payroll_slip.lop_days / working_days / paid_days; payroll_slip_detail.calculation_basis)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme"; employee "Arun" with monthly_basic = 22,000.00, working_days_in_month = 22.
- May 2026 attendance: 5 absent days, of which 2 are covered by approved leave and 3 are unapproved absences.
- Attendance + leave for May 2026 finalized; structure assigned.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| monthly_basic | 22,000.00 | numeric(18,2) |
| working_days_in_month | 22 | BR-2 denominator |
| daily_rate | 1,000.00 | 22000 / 22 |
| absent_days_total | 5 | raw absences |
| approved_leave_days | 2 | excluded from LOP |
| LOP_days | 3 | 5 - 2 (unapproved only) |
| Expected LOP amount | 3,000.00 | 1000 * 3 |
| Expected paid_days | 19 | 22 - 3 |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Run payroll for May 2026; inspect Arun's slip. | `lop_days` = 3.00 (NOT 5 -- the 2 approved-leave days are excluded, BR-2). |
| 2 | Verify daily_rate basis. | LOP detail line shows basis "22000 / 22 * 3 = 3000.00"; daily_rate = 1000.00. |
| 3 | Verify LOP reduces pay. | Net salary is reduced by 3,000.00 relative to a no-LOP baseline; `paid_days` = 19.00, `working_days` = 22.00. |
| 4 | Boundary: employee with 0 unapproved absences (all absences approved leave). | `lop_days` = 0.00; no LOP deduction; full pay. |
| 5 | Boundary: employee absent every working day with no approved leave (LOP_days = 22). | LOP = full monthly_basic; net does not go negative (floored at 0 unless other earnings/adjustments apply); no exception thrown. |

## 6. Postconditions
- LOP is computed only on unapproved absences using the BR-2 formula; approved leave is honored; slip fields reflect LOP.
