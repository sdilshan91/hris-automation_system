# TC-PAY-009-07: Year-End Tax Statements -- cumulative income/deductions/tax across all fiscal-year runs; per-employee PDF + bulk ZIP (AC-3, BR-3)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify AC-3 / FR-1f / FR-7 / BR-3: HR selects "Year-End Tax Statements" for fiscal year 2025-2026; the system generates an individual tax statement (PDF) per employee showing month-wise income, deductions, and total tax paid, with cumulative totals across ALL payroll runs in the fiscal year -- including adjustments and arrears (BR-3); statements are available as a bulk ZIP download. The cumulative totals must reconcile to the sum of the employee's monthly payslips (Test Hint #3).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-009
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-3
- Functional Requirements: FR-1f, FR-2, FR-7
- Non-Functional Requirements: NFR-2
- Business Rules: BR-3, BR-5
- Data Requirements: S7

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" fiscal year Apr 2025-Mar 2026; Finalized runs for all 12 months exist for employee `emp-X` (incl. a bonus adjustment in Dec 2025 and an arrears line in Feb 2026).
- HR user `hr1` with `Payroll.*.All`.
- NOTE (CONDITIONAL): full 12-month fiscal-year data depends on Finalized US-PAY-003 runs spanning the FY and on US-PAY-007 adjustments/arrears existing. Where a complete FY is not yet seeded, this TC asserts the cumulative-aggregation logic over the available Finalized months and the per-employee-PDF + ZIP packaging; QuestPDF tax-statement template fidelity is asserted against available data and noted as DEFERRED where 12-month seed data is absent.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| FY | 2025-2026 (Apr-Mar) | BR-5 |
| emp-X | 12 monthly payslips + Dec bonus + Feb arrears | cumulative source |
| Expected total tax | sum(monthly tax paid) | BR-3 reconciliation |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As hr1, select "Year-End Tax Statements"; FY = 2025-2026; Generate. | The job produces one PDF per employee with month-wise income, deductions, and total tax paid (AC-3, FR-1f). |
| 2 | Open emp-X's statement; inspect the month-wise rows. | 12 month rows (Apr-Mar) each showing income, deductions, tax; arranged in fiscal-year order (BR-5). |
| 3 | Inspect the cumulative totals on the statement. | Total income/deductions/tax equal the sum of emp-X's 12 monthly payslips, INCLUDING the Dec bonus and Feb arrears (BR-3); reconciles to TC-PAY-009-04's payslip-derived figures. |
| 4 | Confirm bulk download. | A single ZIP containing all employees' statement PDFs is available for download (FR-7). |
| 5 | Generate for a partial-year employee (joined mid-FY). | The statement covers only the active months; cumulative totals span only those months, not a fabricated full year (BR-3, BR-7). |
| 6 | Trigger generation for ~5,000 employees. | Generation runs asynchronously via Hangfire and is asserted to enqueue + produce the ZIP; the < 15-min SLA (NFR-2) is measured under TC-PAY-009-10 in a seeded env. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Per-employee year-end tax statement PDFs with reconciled cumulative FY totals (incl. adjustments/arrears) are produced and bulk-downloadable as a ZIP; partial-year cases handled correctly.
