# TC-PAY-009-06: Terminated employees included in historical reports for active periods (BR-7); tenant fiscal-year start respected (BR-5)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-7 / BR-5: a terminated employee must still appear in historical reports for the periods during which they were active (and be excluded only from periods after their termination); and reports honour the tenant's configured fiscal-year start month (e.g. April for India, January for US) when grouping periods, computing fiscal-year-to-date figures, and selecting comparison ranges.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-009
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1
- Functional Requirements: FR-1a, FR-1b, FR-3
- Business Rules: BR-5, BR-7
- Data Requirements: S7

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" fiscal-year start = April (FY 2025-2026 = Apr 2025..Mar 2026).
- Employee `emp-T` was active and paid in Apr+May 2026 then terminated effective 31-May-2026 (so excluded from the June 2026 run).
- Finalized runs exist for Apr, May, June 2026.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| emp-T | active Apr+May, terminated 31-May | historical inclusion |
| Fiscal year | Apr-Mar (acme config) | BR-5 |
| Periods | Apr, May, June 2026 | inclusion boundary |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Generate the May 2026 Payroll Summary + Register. | emp-T appears with their May pay (active period); included in May totals + their department breakdown (BR-7). |
| 2 | Generate the April 2026 report. | emp-T appears (active period); included (BR-7). |
| 3 | Generate the June 2026 report. | emp-T does NOT appear (terminated before the period); June employee_count excludes them (BR-7). |
| 4 | Generate a fiscal-year-to-date report for FY 2025-2026. | Periods are grouped Apr-Mar (not Jan-Dec); emp-T's Apr+May contribution is included in FYTD figures (BR-5). |
| 5 | Switch a second tenant to a Jan-Dec fiscal year and generate its FYTD report. | That tenant's FYTD groups Jan-Dec; acme's Apr-Mar grouping is unaffected (BR-5, per-tenant config). |
| 6 | Run the variance report for a range spanning emp-T's termination. | emp-T shows a decrease/drop-off only for the post-termination period, not a data error (BR-7). |

## 6. Postconditions
- Terminated employees remain in historical reports for their active periods and drop off afterward; report period grouping follows each tenant's fiscal-year start.
