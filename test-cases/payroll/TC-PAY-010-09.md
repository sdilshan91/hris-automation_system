# TC-PAY-010-09: Encashment eligibility (encashable type + over carry-forward only, BR-6); working days from shift calendar not flat 30 (BR-8); notice-period 2x LOP (BR-7) (negative/boundary)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-6, BR-8, and BR-7: (a) leave encashment is only available for leave types with encashment ENABLED and only for the balance EXCEEDING the carry-forward limit -- non-encashable types and the within-carry-forward portion are rejected; (b) working days for the daily-rate denominator come from the employee's shift calendar (e.g. 21 or 24 across departments), never a flat 30; (c) employees on notice period have their LOP deduction doubled per tenant notice-period policy.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-010
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1, AC-3
- Functional Requirements: FR-3, FR-5
- Business Rules: BR-6, BR-7, BR-8

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" Active; Payroll module enabled.
- Leave types: "Annual" (encashment ENABLED, carry-forward limit 10 days), "Sick" (encashment DISABLED).
- Employee A balance: 14 Annual days (4 over the 10-day carry-forward limit), daily_rate 1,000.
- Employee B: shift calendar gives 21 working days for the period (not 30); monthly_basic 21,000 -> daily_rate 1,000.
- Employee C: on notice period; tenant policy = 2x LOP during notice.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Annual (encashable, CF=10) balance | 14 | only 4 over CF encashable (BR-6) |
| Sick (non-encashable) | any | not encashable (BR-6) |
| Working days (B) | 21 (shift calendar) | not flat 30 (BR-8) |
| Notice-period LOP factor (C) | 2x | BR-7 |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | HR triggers encashment of Annual leave for Employee A (balance 14, CF limit 10). | Only the 4 days over carry-forward are encashable; encashment_days capped at 4, amount = 4 * 1,000 = 4,000 (BR-6). |
| 2 | HR attempts to encash the "Sick" (non-encashable) type. | Rejected -- encashment not available for types without encashment enabled (BR-6). |
| 3 | Run payroll for Employee B whose shift calendar yields 21 working days. | daily_rate = 21,000 / 21 = 1,000 using shift-calendar working days, NOT 21,000 / 30 (BR-8). |
| 4 | Boundary: a department with a 6-day-week shift (e.g. 24 working days). | daily_rate uses 24 as denominator; LOP scales accordingly -- per-department shift patterns respected (BR-8). |
| 5 | Run payroll for Employee C (on notice period) with 2 uncovered absent days. | LOP deduction = 2 * daily_rate * 2 (notice-period 2x factor) per tenant policy (BR-7). |
| 6 | Verify reconciliation across all three. | Each payslip's encashment / daily-rate / notice-LOP reconciles to its rule; no flat-30 assumption anywhere. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Encashment limited to encashable types over carry-forward; daily rate uses shift-calendar working days; notice-period LOP doubled per policy.
