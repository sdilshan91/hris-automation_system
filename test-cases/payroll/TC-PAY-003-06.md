# TC-PAY-003-06: Pro-rated salary for mid-month joiner and mid-month separator (BR-4 / BR-5)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-4 and BR-5: an employee who JOINED mid-month receives a salary pro-rated on actual working days from the date of joining; an employee who SEPARATED mid-month receives a salary pro-rated on actual working days up to the last working day. Pro-ration is reflected in `working_days` / `paid_days` and in the per-component amounts, and interacts correctly with LOP (LOP applies only within the eligible window).

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-003
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-5
- Business Rules: BR-4, BR-5
- Data Requirements: S7 (payroll_slip.working_days / paid_days)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme"; May 2026 has 22 working days; daily_rate derived per BR-2.
- Joiner "Priya": date_of_joining = 2026-05-16 (10 working days remaining in May).
- Separator "Sanjay": last_working_day = 2026-05-15 (11 working days worked in May).
- A full-month control employee "Ravi" present all 22 working days.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Working days in month | 22 | May 2026 |
| Priya DOJ | 2026-05-16 | Joiner |
| Priya eligible working days | 10 | From DOJ to month end |
| Sanjay LWD | 2026-05-15 | Separator |
| Sanjay eligible working days | 11 | Month start to LWD |
| Monthly gross (each) | 44,000.00 | For pro-rata math |
| Priya expected gross | 20,000.00 | 44000 * 10/22 |
| Sanjay expected gross | 22,000.00 | 44000 * 11/22 |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Run payroll for May 2026; inspect Priya's slip (joiner). | `working_days` reflects 10 eligible days; gross pro-rated to ~20,000.00 (44000 * 10/22); detail lines carry a pro-ration basis. |
| 2 | Inspect Sanjay's slip (separator). | `working_days` reflects 11 eligible days; gross pro-rated to ~22,000.00 (44000 * 11/22). |
| 3 | Inspect Ravi's slip (full month control). | Full gross 44,000.00; not pro-rated. |
| 4 | Combine with LOP: give Priya 1 unapproved absence inside her eligible window. | LOP applies only to her eligible window (daily_rate * 1); paid_days = eligible days - 1; no double-counting of the pre-joining period as LOP. |
| 5 | Boundary: joiner on the last working day of the month. | 1 eligible working day pro-ration; no division/rounding error; net >= 0. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Mid-month joiners/separators are pro-rated on actual eligible working days; pro-ration composes correctly with LOP.
