# TC-PAY-006-07: Wage ceiling evaluated per pay period; progressive tax considers year-to-date cumulative income

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-8 / BR-5: (1) the social-security wage ceiling is evaluated **per pay period** -- monthly ceiling = annual ceiling / 12 (or per the statutory rule), so a single month's contribution never uses the full annual ceiling; and (2) progressive income tax may consider **year-to-date cumulative income** so that as YTD income crosses a slab boundary mid-year, the marginal slab rate is applied correctly for that period rather than treating each month in isolation.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-006
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1, AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-2, FR-5
- Business Rules: BR-5 (YTD cumulative), BR-8 (wage ceiling per period)
- Data Requirements: S7 (social_security_rule.wage_ceiling_annual, tax_slab)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" with EPF rule wage_ceiling_annual 180,000 (monthly 15,000) and tax slabs configured.
- A monthly payroll period and prior-period (YTD) totals available for the employee.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Annual wage ceiling | 180,000 | monthly = 15,000 (BR-8) |
| Monthly basic | 20,000 | capped to 15,000/period |
| YTD income before period | 480,000 | near 500K slab edge |
| Period income | 50,000 | crosses 500K into 20% band (BR-5) |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Compute EPF for a single month with monthly basic 20,000 and annual ceiling 180,000. | The contribution uses the monthly ceiling 15,000 (180,000/12), not the annual 180,000; employee EPF = 1,800 (BR-8). |
| 2 | Compute EPF for 12 monthly periods cumulatively. | Total annual EPF = 12 * 1,800 = 21,600; per-period ceiling applied each month -- no single month consumes the full annual cap. |
| 3 | For tax, set YTD income before this period = 480,000 and period income = 50,000. | The portion of period income up to the 500K cumulative boundary is taxed at 5% and the portion above at 20% per BR-5 (YTD-aware marginal application). |
| 4 | Verify the cumulative method matches the slab boundary crossing. | The period that crosses 500K splits across the 5% and 20% bands based on YTD position, not flat per-month slabbing. |
| 5 | Boundary: a period entirely within one YTD band. | The whole period income is taxed at that band's marginal rate; no spurious band switch. |
| 6 | Boundary: wage ceiling expressed as a custom statutory rule (not annual/12). | The ceiling-per-period follows the configured statutory rule rather than the default /12 (BR-8 "or as per statutory rules"). |

## 6. Postconditions
- No state change (calculation only); per-period ceiling and YTD-aware progressive tax verified.
