# TC-PAY-005-04: Payslips from non-Finalized runs (Queued/Processing/ReviewPending/Approved) are hidden from the employee view (negative / BR-1)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-1 / FR-2: the employee's My Payslips list and detail/download endpoints expose ONLY payslips whose payroll run is in Finalized status. Slips belonging to runs in Queued, Processing, ReviewPending, or Approved status are not listed, not returned by detail, and not downloadable -- they become visible only once the run transitions to Finalized.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-005
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1 (implied: list = Finalized only)
- Functional Requirements: FR-2
- Business Rules: BR-1
- Data Requirements: S7 (payroll_run.status, payroll_slip)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme"; Employee EMP-001 with `Payroll.Read.Self`.
- EMP-001 has slips across runs: Apr 2026 (Finalized), Jun 2026 (ReviewPending), Jul 2026 (Approved, not yet Finalized).

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Finalized slip | Apr 2026 | visible |
| ReviewPending slip | Jun 2026 | hidden (BR-1) |
| Approved slip | Jul 2026 | hidden (BR-1) |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As EMP-001, open My Payslips. | Only Apr 2026 (Finalized) is listed; Jun (ReviewPending) and Jul (Approved) are absent (BR-1, FR-2). |
| 2 | Attempt to fetch the Jun 2026 (ReviewPending) payslip detail by its id directly via the my-payslips detail endpoint. | 404 Not Found (filtered as not-Finalized); no detail returned. |
| 3 | Attempt to download the Jun 2026 PDF via my-payslips/{id}/pdf. | 404 Not Found / not-available; no PDF served. |
| 4 | Transition the Jun 2026 run to Finalized (admin action, out of band). | Re-open My Payslips: Jun 2026 now appears in the list. |
| 5 | Re-fetch Jun 2026 detail + PDF after finalization. | Detail and PDF now return successfully (the slip became visible only on Finalized). |
| 6 | Confirm the Approved (Jul) slip remains hidden. | Jul 2026 (Approved, not Finalized) is still absent from the list. |

## 6. Postconditions
- The employee sees a payslip only after its run is Finalized; pre-Finalized slips never leak through list, detail, or download.
