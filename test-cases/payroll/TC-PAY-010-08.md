# TC-PAY-010-08: Public holiday worked counts as overtime at the holiday OT rate (2x) (boundary, BR-5)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-5 / FR-4: hours worked on a public holiday are treated as overtime at the holiday OT multiplier (typically 2x), distinct from the standard OT multiplier. With 8 holiday-worked hours at base hourly 200 and a 2x holiday rate, the overtime_amount for those hours = 8 * 200 * 2 = 3,200; a mix of standard OT and holiday OT applies each multiplier to the right hours.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-PAY-010
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-1, FR-4
- Business Rules: BR-4, BR-5

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant "acme" Active; Payroll module enabled.
- Tenant OT config: standard multiplier 1.5x, holiday multiplier 2.0x; base hourly 200.
- Attendance for the period FINALIZED. Employee worked 8 approved hours on a recognized public holiday (plus, in the mixed case, 10 standard approved OT hours).

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Holiday-worked hours (approved) | 8 | 2x rate (BR-5) |
| Holiday multiplier | 2.0x | tenant config |
| base hourly | 200 | |
| Expected holiday OT amount | 8 * 200 * 2 = 3,200 | boundary |
| Mixed standard OT | 10 @ 1.5x | 10 * 200 * 1.5 = 3,000 |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Run payroll for the employee with 8 approved public-holiday-worked hours. | Those hours are treated as OT at the 2x holiday rate; holiday OT amount = 3,200 (BR-5). |
| 2 | Verify the holiday rate, not the standard rate, was applied. | 2.0x used (3,200), not 1.5x (which would be 2,400). |
| 3 | Mixed case: 10 standard approved OT hours + 8 holiday hours. | Standard OT = 3,000 (1.5x) and holiday OT = 3,200 (2x); total OT amount = 6,200; each block uses its own multiplier. |
| 4 | Negative: holiday hours worked but NOT approved. | Excluded (BR-4) -- holiday work still requires approval to be eligible. |
| 5 | Verify gross/net + reconciliation. | Gross/net include the correct multi-rate OT; payslip totals reconcile. |

## 6. Postconditions
- Public-holiday work is paid as OT at the holiday multiplier; standard and holiday OT blocks each apply the correct rate; approval still required.
