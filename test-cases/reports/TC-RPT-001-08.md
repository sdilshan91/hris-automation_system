# TC-RPT-001-08: Manager team-scope (Reports.View.Team) sees only direct reports vs HR Officer full-tenant (BR-2)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify BR-2: a Manager holding `Reports.View.Team` sees report data limited to their direct
reports only, while an HR Officer holding `Reports.View.All` sees the full tenant. The team-scope
restriction is enforced on the data, not just the displayed total. Anchored to the Test Hint
"log in as a Manager; verify only direct reports' data is included".

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-RPT-001
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2, AC-3
- Functional Requirements: FR-7 (tenant context)
- Business Rules: BR-2 (Team vs All scope)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant A active.
- `mgr1` holds `Reports.View.Team` and has exactly 4 direct reports (one of which is `probation`).
- `hr1` holds `Reports.View.All`; Tenant A has a known full active count (e.g. 43).
- At least one of `mgr1`'s direct reports separated within the turnover window.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| manager | mgr1 | Reports.View.Team, 4 direct reports |
| hr officer | hr1 | Reports.View.All, sees 43 |
| direct reports of mgr1 | 4 | the only rows mgr1 may see |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As `mgr1`, generate Headcount Summary | Total reflects ONLY the 4 direct reports (and their BR-4 active subset), NOT the tenant's 43 |
| 2 | As `mgr1`, inspect the by-department/employee breakdown | Contains only the manager's direct reports; no other employees enumerated |
| 3 | As `mgr1`, generate Employee Turnover | Separations counted only among direct reports |
| 4 | As `mgr1`, attempt to widen scope by passing department_ids covering the whole tenant | Result still constrained to direct reports — Team scope is not bypassable via filters |
| 5 | As `hr1` (control), generate the same Headcount Summary | Total = full tenant 43 — confirms All vs Team scope differ on the same data |
| 6 | As `mgr1`, attempt to query a peer manager's report data via API param manipulation | Denied / constrained to own direct reports (no escalation to another manager's team) |

## 6. Postconditions
- Team scope strictly limits data to direct reports; All scope sees the whole tenant.
