# TC-RPT-002-06: Filters apply (department/leave-type/employee/shift) and aggregate drill-down works (FR-2, FR-6)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify the leave/attendance reports honour the documented filters — date range, department
(multi-select), employee, leave type, shift — and that drill-down from an aggregate (e.g. clicking a
department bar) reveals the underlying individual records (FR-6). Validates FR-2 and FR-6.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-RPT-002
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1, AC-3
- Functional Requirements: FR-2 (filters), FR-6 (drill-down)
- Business Rules: BR-1

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant A active; `hr1` authenticated with `Reports.View.All`.
- At least 2 departments, 2 leave types, 2 shifts, and multiple employees with known data.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| department_ids | [Engineering] | restrict to one dept |
| leave_type_ids | [Annual] | restrict to one type |
| employee_id | empA | single-employee restriction |
| shift_ids | [Day shift] | overtime/attendance filter |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Generate Leave Utilization filtered to department=Engineering | Only Engineering employees contribute; totals = Engineering subset only |
| 2 | Add leave_type filter = Annual | Charts/tables restricted to Annual leave; other types excluded |
| 3 | Set employee_id = empA | Report scopes to empA only |
| 4 | Generate Attendance Summary with shift_ids = [Day shift] | Only Day-shift attendance/overtime included |
| 5 | On the department grouped-bar, click the Engineering bar (drill-down, FR-6) | A slide-out panel lists the individual Engineering employees' records behind the aggregate |
| 6 | Clear all filters; regenerate | Full-tenant figures return (superset of every filtered subset) |
| 7 | Confirm filter combinations are AND-composed | Multiple filters narrow cumulatively; no filter silently ignored |

## 6. Postconditions
- Filters and drill-down behave per FR-2 / FR-6; no data mutated.
