# TC-RPT-003-03: Department-wise Salary Distribution — stacked bar by component + per-dept totals & counts (AC-2)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify the "Department-wise Salary Distribution" report renders a stacked bar chart of total salary
cost per department broken down by earnings component (basic, HRA, allowances), with a data table
below showing per-department totals and employee counts. Validates AC-2, FR-1, FR-5, and the Test
Hint "assign employees to 3 departments; verify stacked bar shows correct salary breakdown per
department".

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-RPT-003
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-2
- Functional Requirements: FR-1, FR-2, FR-5
- Business Rules: BR-1, BR-2

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant A active; `hr1` authenticated with `Payroll.Export`.
- A FINALIZED 2026-03 run with employees assigned to 3 departments with KNOWN component splits, e.g.:
  - Engineering (20 emp): basic=80,000, HRA=24,000, allowances=16,000 → total 120,000.
  - Sales (18 emp): basic=63,000, HRA=18,900, allowances=8,100 → total 90,000.
  - HR (12 emp): basic=28,000, HRA=8,400, allowances=3,600 → total 40,000.
- Grand totals reconcile with TC-RPT-003-01's gross.
