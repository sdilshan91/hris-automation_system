# TC-RPT-001-03: Employee Turnover rate = separations / avg headcount * 100 (BR-3 calculation correctness)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify the Employee Turnover report computes the turnover rate exactly as
(Separations in Period / Average Headcount in Period) x 100 per BR-3, and renders separations,
voluntary vs involuntary counts/percentages, monthly trend, by-department breakdown, and average
tenure of departed employees. Anchored to the Test Hint: 100 employees, 10 terminated -> rate = 10%.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-RPT-001
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-3
- Functional Requirements: FR-1, FR-3
- Business Rules: BR-3 (turnover formula), BR-4 (separated classification)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant A active; `hr1` authenticated with `Reports.View.All`.
- Period under test (e.g. last 12 months) starts with 100 active employees; during the period
  10 are terminated (status -> `terminated`), no hires -> average headcount over the period is
  defined per the implementation's convention (e.g. (start 100 + end 90)/2 = 95, OR period-average
  100 if computed on opening headcount).
- The 10 separations have a known voluntary/involuntary split (e.g. 6 resigned = voluntary,
  4 terminated = involuntary) and known tenures for the average-tenure check.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| report_type | employee_turnover | |
| date_from / date_to | last 12 months | AC-3 window |
| separations | 10 | per Test Hint |
| simple-case expected rate | 10% | 10 / 100 * 100 (opening-headcount convention) |
| avg-headcount-convention rate | document actual | if avg = 95, rate = 10/95*100 = 10.53% |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Generate Employee Turnover for the last 12 months | Report renders: total separations, voluntary vs involuntary count+%, monthly trend line chart, by-department horizontal bar chart, avg tenure of departed (AC-3) |
| 2 | Read total separations | = 10 |
| 3 | Read the turnover rate | Equals separations / average-headcount * 100 per BR-3. For the 100-emp/10-terminated Test Hint this resolves to 10% on the opening-headcount basis; if the implementation uses (start+end)/2 = 95 the expected value is 10.53% — record which average-headcount convention the system uses and assert against it (do NOT accept an unexplained third value) |
| 4 | Verify voluntary vs involuntary | Counts (6 / 4) and percentages (60% / 40%) match the seeded split |
| 5 | Inspect the monthly trend line chart | Separations distributed across months matching seed; sum of monthly points = 10 |
| 6 | Inspect the by-department bar chart | Per-department separation counts sum to 10 |
| 7 | Read average tenure of departed employees | Matches the mean of the 10 seeded tenures within rounding tolerance |

## 6. Postconditions
- Turnover figures verifiably derived from BR-3; average-headcount convention documented.
