# TC-RPT-005-08: Upcoming Birthdays/Anniversaries within next 7 days (BR-4); Quick Actions surfaces top 5 most urgent items (BR-6) (AC-1/2, FR-6/7)

## 1. Test Objective
Verify two windowing/limit business rules: (a) the Upcoming Birthdays/Anniversaries widget includes only
events occurring within the NEXT 7 days inclusive (BR-4), excluding events 8+ days out and past events; and
(b) the Quick Actions panel surfaces at most the TOP 5 most urgent actionable items (BR-6). Validates AC-1,
AC-2, FR-6, FR-7, BR-4, BR-6.

## 2. Related Requirements
- User Story: US-RPT-005
- Acceptance Criteria: AC-1 (HR upcoming birthdays/anniversaries), AC-2 (Manager quick actions)
- Functional Requirements: FR-6 (activity/upcoming-events), FR-7 (Quick Actions panel)
- Business Rules: BR-4 (events within next 7 days), BR-6 (Quick Actions top 5)

## 3. Preconditions
- Tenant A active; HR Officer `hr1` and Manager `mgr1` authenticated. Report-date = 2026-06-17.
- Seed employees with: a birthday on 2026-06-18 (in window, +1d), a work anniversary on 2026-06-24
  (in window, +7d boundary), a birthday on 2026-06-25 (out of window, +8d), and a birthday on 2026-06-16 (past, excluded).
- Seed 8 actionable items for `mgr1` with differing urgency for the Quick Actions limit test.

## 4. Test Data
| Field | Value | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| event +1d (2026-06-18) | included | within 7 days |
| event +7d (2026-06-24) | included | boundary inclusive (BR-4) |
| event +8d (2026-06-25) | excluded | outside window |
| event -1d (2026-06-16) | excluded | past event |
| quick-action items seeded | 8 | only top 5 shown (BR-6) |

## 5. Test Steps
| Step | Action | Expected Result |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | As `hr1`, read `upcoming_birthdays` widget | `items[]` include the +1d and +7d events; the +8d and the past event are EXCLUDED (BR-4) |
| 2 | Confirm the +7d boundary is inclusive | the 2026-06-24 anniversary (exactly 7 days out) is present (BR-4) |
| 3 | Confirm past events are excluded | the 2026-06-16 birthday does not appear (BR-4) |
| 4 | As `mgr1`, read the `quick_actions` panel | exactly 5 items returned even though 8 actionable items exist (BR-6) |
| 5 | Confirm the 5 are the most urgent | the 5 shown are ordered/selected by urgency (the 3 least urgent are dropped) (BR-6) |
| 6 | Reduce mgr1's actionable items to 3 and re-read | all 3 shown (top-5 is a cap, not a fixed count) (BR-6) |

## 6. Postconditions
- Upcoming-events widget windowed to next 7 days; Quick Actions capped at the 5 most urgent.
